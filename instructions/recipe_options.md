## Difficulty Gotchas (Global)
[Flag what makes an option hard: skill spikes, coordination load, or narrow windows. Add quick mitigations.]
- **Skill bottlenecks:** precise knife work, tight emulsions, sugar stages, dough shaping, fine brunoise, fish butchery. *Mitigate:* pre-cut, practice on a small batch, or pick an easier variant.
- **Heat control traps:** high-heat sears with low-smoke oils; pan temperature swings; shallow-fry vs deep-fry stability. *Mitigate:* choose higher smoke-point fats; preheat adequately; use a thermometer.
- **Timing/parallelization:** multiple pans/timers, sauce + starch synchronization, resting windows. *Mitigate:* strict mise en place; stagger starts; add a holding step.
- **Texture-sensitive steps:** overmixing batters, curdling dairy, broken emulsions. *Mitigate:* mix just-to-combine; temper dairy; re-emulsify off heat with water.
- **Equipment constraints:** limited burners/oven space; missing tools (scale, probe). *Mitigate:* batch in waves; swap vessels; add parchment/racks.
- **Recovery options:** salt too high; sauce too thin/thick; crust too pale. *Mitigate:* dilute/acid, reduce/loosen, finish at higher heat.
