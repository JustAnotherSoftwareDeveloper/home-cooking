# Spicy Frozen-Tofu Mapo-Style Shredded Pork & Veg Crumble (Doubled Batch)

Punchy, numbing, freezer-ready bowl base. Cook once, portion flat, freeze. Later: microwave a block and ladle over **freshly cooked white rice** (rice is not part of the frozen base).

---

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** ~10-12 single-serve portions (1-1 1/4 cups each)
- **Active time:** ~60 minutes (plus tofu freeze/press and cooling)
- **Fridge:** 3-4 days (chill fast in shallow containers)
- **Freezer:** best quality ~3-4 months (portion flat, ~1 in thick)

---

## Ingredients (Doubled)

### Proteins
- 2 lb boneless pork shoulder, trimmed and cut into **1/4 in x 1.5-2 in matchsticks**
- 2 blocks extra-firm tofu (~14 oz each), **frozen >= 8 hr, thawed, pressed 20-30 min** [pre-prep]

### Pork Marinade
- 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce  
- 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry)  
- 2 tsp cornstarch  
- 1/2 tsp baking soda (**do not exceed**)

### Vegetables (freezer-tolerant; cut for reheat)
- 16 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced  
- 16 oz green beans, trimmed, cut 1 in (frozen OK)  
- 2 red bell peppers, **1/2 in dice**  
- 8 scallions, sliced (**reserve most green tops for serving**, not freezing)

### Aromatics & Spicy Base
- 4 tbsp neutral oil (plus **+1 tbsp on standby** if paste looks dry)
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp minced fresh ginger
- 6 tbsp doubanjiang (Sichuan chili bean paste)
- 2 tbsp douchi (fermented black beans), rinsed, roughly chopped
- **6 tsp** Sichuan chili flakes (heat baseline; finish heat with chili oil)
- 1 tbsp sugar

### Sauce, Finish, Thickener
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken stock (or water)
- **3 tbsp** low-sodium soy sauce to start (add up to **+1 tbsp at the end** if needed)
- **4-6 tsp** ground Sichuan pepper (start 4, add to taste at the end)
- 4 tbsp cornstarch + 6 tbsp cold water (slurry; you **will not** use it all)
- 2-4 tbsp chili oil, to taste
- Kosher salt, only if needed

---

## How to Prep the Pork (explicit)
- **Trim:** Remove big fat caps/silverskin; leave some marbling.
- **Firm for slicing:** Chill very cold or **par-freeze 20-30 min**.
- **Slice across the grain:** Cut 1/4 in planks, then matchsticks (1.5-2 in).
- **Marinate 15-30 min (cap it):** Toss with **soy + Shaoxing + cornstarch + baking soda**.
- **Light blot before sear:** If very wet, pat once so it browns instead of steams.

---

## Instructions

1) **Tofu & Pork [pre-prep]**  
   - **Tofu:** Freeze unopened blocks >= 8 hr; thaw. Press 20-30 min; crumble into **~1/2 in** chunks (chewy, absorbent).  
   - **Pork:** Prep/marinate as above (15-30 min, not longer).

2) **Batch-Sear the Pork (browning matters)**  
   - **Pan:** Large wok or 12-14 in skillet, **medium-high**, **2 tbsp** oil.  
   - **Batch 1:** Half the pork, **1-2 min** undisturbed, then stir-fry **3-4 min** to **just cooked** with browned edges. Remove to bowl.  
   - **Batch 2:** **+1 tbsp** oil, repeat. **Keep the flavored fat in the pan.**

3) **Bloom the Chili-Bean Base (do not scorch)**  
   - Heat **medium**.  
   - **Garlic + ginger:** **~30 sec**.  
   - **Doubanjiang + douchi + chili flakes:** **45-60 sec** until oil turns **deep red** and fragrant.  
   - If paste looks dry, **+1 tbsp oil**.  
   - Stir in **1 tbsp sugar**.

4) **Vegetables (slightly undercook)**  
   - Add **mushrooms, green beans, bell peppers**.  
   - Stir-fry **4-5 min** to **just-tender**.  
   - If dry, splash **a few tbsp stock**.  
   - *(Small pan? Do veg in two quick rounds.)*

5) **Combine & Build the Sauce**  
   - Return **pork + juices**.  
   - Fold in **crumbled tofu** and **most scallion whites** (save greens).  
   - Add **4 cups stock** + **3 tbsp soy**.  
   - **Strong simmer 6-8 min** to integrate and **reduce slightly**.

6) **Thicken Gradually (slurry is not linear)**  
   - Stir slurry.  
   - Add **~70%** in a thin stream while stirring.  
   - **Simmer 2-3 min** until **glossy** and **clings** like a loose chili ragu.  
   - If still thin, add **1-2 tsp** slurry at a time; **simmer 1 min** after each.

7) **Finish Heat, Numbing, and Salt**  
   - Stir in **4 tsp** ground Sichuan pepper; taste. Add more in **1/2-tsp** pops (up to **6 tsp**).  
   - Add **2-4 tbsp** chili oil to set final heat.  
   - Taste salt; if under-seasoned, add up to **+1 tbsp soy** now.

8) **Cool Fast, Portion Flat, Freeze**  
   - Spread into **shallow** containers or sheet pans to stop cooking.  
   - Counter only until heavy steaming subsides (**<= 30 min**), then **refrigerate to chill fully**.  
   - Portion **1-1 1/4 cups** per **microwave-safe** container; **flatten to ~1 in** thick.  
   - Label; freeze (best quality ~3-4 months).

9) **Reheat & Serve**  
   - From frozen: cover-loose, microwave **2-3 min**, stir, then **1-2 min** bursts until **piping hot and bubbling** (aim **>= 165 deg F** in center).  
   - Spoon over **fresh white rice**; finish with **scallion greens** and a thread of **chili oil**.
