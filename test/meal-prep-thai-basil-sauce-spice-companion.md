# Thai Basil Companion for the Base Recipe

Scaled for the full **5-cup dry-rice batch** and approximately **14 cups of beef, carrots, and green beans** in the base recipe.

## Rice Seasoning

Add the following before pressure cooking:

* 4 cups water
* 1 cup unsalted beef stock
* 2 tbsp avocado oil, replacing the ghee
* 1 1/2 tsp ground ginger
* 1 tsp ground coriander
* 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
* 1/2 tsp makrut lime leaf powder
* 6 g fine salt, approximately 1 tsp

Cook using the base recipe's existing pressure time and release method.

## Shortcut Thai Basil Sauce

* 1 full 7 oz jar Por Kwan Chili Paste with Holy Basil
* 1/3 cup Thai oyster sauce
* 2 tbsp Golden Mountain seasoning sauce
* 1 tbsp fish sauce
* 1 tbsp Thai black soy sauce
* 1/2 cup unsalted beef cooking liquid or water
* 1 to 2 tbsp Thai bird's-eye chile powder

  * 1 tbsp: hot
  * 2 tbsp: very hot
* Optional: 1 tsp palm sugar or brown sugar

### Instructions

1. **Whisk** all sauce ingredients together in a small pot.
2. **Simmer over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes**, stirring frequently, until the sauce is unified and slightly reduced.
3. **Fold** the sauce into the finished beef, carrots, and green beans.
4. Taste the sauced mixture together with an equal amount of rice before making adjustments.

## Base-Recipe Salt Changes

Because the sauce contains several salty ingredients, change the base seasoning to:

* Rice: **6 g fine salt**
* Beef: **2 g fine salt**
* Carrots: **no added salt**
* Green beans: **no added salt**
