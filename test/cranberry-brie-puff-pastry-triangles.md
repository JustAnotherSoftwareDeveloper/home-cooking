# Cranberry–Brie Puff Pastry Triangles

## Overview
Flaky, buttery puff pastry triangles filled with creamy Brie and a bright cranberry–orange sauce. They bake up crisp and golden with a gooey, tangy center. The whole recipe is built for make-ahead: assemble at home, then bake straight from cold (or frozen) in the host’s oven.

---

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** ~24 small triangles (8–10 appetizer servings)
- **Active Prep:** 35–45 minutes (including sauce)
- **Hands-Off/Rest:** 45–60 minutes (chilling)
- **Bake Time:** 15–20 minutes
- **Total Time:** ~1 hour 45 minutes (largely passive)

## Ingredients

### Cranberry–Orange Sauce  
*(Skip and use 1 cup prepared cranberry sauce if you want the shortcut.)*

- 1 cup fresh cranberries
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup orange juice (from the orange)
- 1 tsp finely grated orange zest
- Pinch kosher salt
- 1–2 tsp honey or maple syrup, to taste (optional)

### Puff Pastry Triangles

- 2 sheets all-butter frozen puff pastry, thawed in the fridge until pliable but still cold
- 8 oz **firm, well-chilled Brie**, cut into 24 small cubes (about ¾–1 inch)
- ½–¾ cup cranberry–orange sauce (from above) or prepared cranberry sauce
- ¼–½ cup finely chopped pecans or walnuts (optional)
- 1 large egg
- 1 tbsp water
- All-purpose flour, for dusting work surface
- Flaky sea salt, fresh thyme leaves, and/or extra orange zest (optional garnish)

---

## Instructions

### A. Make the Cranberry–Orange Sauce (Up to 3 Days Ahead)

1. **Combine ingredients**  
   In a small saucepan, combine:
   - 1 cup cranberries  
   - ¼ cup sugar  
   - ¼ cup orange juice  
   - 1 tsp orange zest  
   - Pinch kosher salt  

2. **Cook until jammy**  
   Bring to a simmer over medium heat. Cook 8–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until:
   - Most cranberries have burst  
   - The mixture is thick and glossy, and a spatula dragged through briefly reveals the bottom of the pan  

3. **Adjust sweetness & cool**  
   Taste; add 1–2 tsp honey or maple syrup if you want it a bit sweeter/richer.  
   Transfer to a shallow dish and cool completely.  
   Once cool, cover and refrigerate for up to 3 days.

---

### B. Prep Brie, Egg Wash & Work Surface

4. **Cube the Brie**  
   Cut the **firm, cold Brie** into 24 small cubes (about ¾–1 inch).  
   Place the cubes back in the fridge so they stay firm while you work.

5. **Make egg wash**  
   In a small bowl, whisk together:
   - 1 large egg  
   - 1 tbsp water  

   Set aside with a pastry brush.

6. **Prepare pans and surface**  
   - Line 1–2 rimmed baking sheets with parchment or silicone mats.  
   - Lightly dust your work surface with flour.

---

### C. Cut, Fill & Shape the Triangles

7. **Roll the first puff pastry sheet**  
   Unfold 1 sheet of cold puff pastry onto the floured surface.  
   Gently roll just enough to even out creases and reach about **⅛–3⁄16 inch** thickness. Avoid over-rolling; you want to preserve layers.

8. **Cut into squares**  
   Trim rough edges if needed, then cut into ~12 squares, **about 2½–3 inches** on each side. Use a ruler if you want consistency.

9. **Add filling**  
   Working with one square at a time:
   - Place **1 Brie cube** slightly off-center.  
   - Spoon **about ½ tsp cranberry–orange sauce** on top (1 tsp max; too much will leak).  
   - Sprinkle a small pinch of chopped nuts over the sauce, if using.

10. **Fold into triangle**  
    Brush the exposed pastry edges lightly with egg wash.  
    Fold the square diagonally over the filling to create a triangle.  
    Press the edges firmly together with your fingers.

11. **Seal & vent**  
    - Crimp the edges with the tines of a fork to reinforce the seal.  
    - Use the tip of a knife to cut a **small slit or “X”** in the top of each triangle to vent steam.

12. **Transfer to tray**  
    Place each triangle on the prepared baking sheet, leaving about 1 inch between pieces.

13. **Repeat with second sheet**  
    Repeat Steps 7–12 with the second puff pastry sheet to yield about **24 triangles** total.

---

### D. Chill (Critical for Good Puff)

14. **Chill before baking or transporting**  
    Place the trays of assembled triangles in the refrigerator for at least **30 minutes**, until the pastry is firm and cold.

    - **Up to 24 hours in the fridge:** Once firm, cover tightly.  
    - **To freeze:** Freeze on the tray until solid, then transfer frozen triangles to a freezer bag or airtight container for up to **1 month**.

---

### E. Bake at the Host’s House

15. **Preheat oven**  
    Preheat oven to **400°F**.  
    Position racks in the upper and lower thirds if using two trays.

16. **Prep for baking**  
    - Arrange chilled or frozen triangles on parchment-lined sheets (give them space).  
    - Brush the tops lightly with egg wash.

17. **Bake until deep golden**  
    Bake for **15–20 minutes**, rotating trays halfway if using two racks.

    - From **fridge-cold**: typically 15–18 minutes.  
    - From **frozen**: add about 2–4 minutes as needed.  

    They’re done when:
    - Triangles are well puffed and **deep golden brown** on top and along the bottom edges.  
    - Any exposed cheese is melted and just starting to brown.

18. **Cool briefly & garnish**  
    Let triangles rest on the baking sheet for about **5 minutes** so the cheese sets slightly.  
    Transfer to a cooling rack or serving platter.  
    Garnish with flaky sea salt, fresh thyme leaves, and/or a little orange zest, if desired.

19. **Serve**  
    Serve warm or at room temperature.  
    If you have extra cranberry–orange sauce, offer it on the side for dipping.

---

## Make-Ahead, Storage & Reheat

- **Unbaked, refrigerated:**  
  Assembled triangles can be kept covered in the fridge up to **24 hours**. Bake from cold at 400°F.

- **Unbaked, frozen:**  
  Freeze assembled triangles on trays, then bag. Bake from frozen at **400°F**, adding a few minutes to the bake time.

- **Baked leftovers:**  
  Store cooled triangles in an airtight container in the fridge for up to **3 days**.

- **Reheat for crispness:**  
  Reheat on a parchment-lined sheet at **350°F for 8–10 minutes**, until hot and re-crisped.  
  Avoid the microwave if possible; it softens the pastry.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix / Next Time |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| Pale or soggy bottoms | Oven not fully preheated; pastry too thick; underbaked | Verify 400°F with full preheat. Bake until **deep golden on the bottom edges**; roll pastry a bit thinner next time. |
| Filling leaks out | Overfilled; edges not sealed; no vent | Use less Brie/sauce, brush edges with egg wash before folding, crimp firmly, always cut a vent slit. |
| Triangles pop open | Dough too warm/soft; no chill before baking | Work with cold pastry; chill assembled triangles at least 30 minutes before baking. |
| Pastry tough instead of flaky | Heavy-handed rolling; too much flour | Roll gently and only as much as needed; brush off excess flour before baking. |
| Cheese/oil pooling & greasy feel | Brie was very soft/overripe or warm going into pastry | Use a **firmer Brie**, keep it **well-chilled**; for softer Brie, chill cubes or assembled triangles briefly in the freezer before baking. |
| Filling feels skimpy | Under-filling or overbaking | Slightly increase Brie/sauce per triangle; pull pastries when golden, not dark brown. |

---

## Notes on Brie Softness & Safety

### Brie Softness / Ripeness
- For puff pastry bakes, test kitchens recommend **firmer, not ultra-ripe Brie**:
  - When cold, it should cut into neat cubes and **hold its shape**.  
  - If it’s already oozy at fridge temperature, it’s too ripe; it tends to leak and render out more fat.
- Use Brie **straight from the fridge** for cubing and assembly.  
- If your Brie is on the softer side, you can:
  - Cube it, then chill the cubes briefly in the freezer (10–15 minutes).  
  - Or chill the **assembled triangles** 30+ minutes (or freeze before baking).  

This gives the puff pastry time to crisp and brown while the Brie melts, without turning into a greasy puddle.

### Safety / Handling
- Keep puff pastry and Brie **cold** as you work; don’t leave dairy-rich fillings at room temp for more than ~2 hours total.  
- Cool the cranberry sauce completely before using it as filling to avoid melting the pastry layers prematurely.  
- Refrigerate any baked leftovers within 2 hours and reheat until hot through before serving again.
