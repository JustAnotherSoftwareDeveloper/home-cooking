# Instant Pot Cheerwine Pulled Pork (Hybrid Sauce: Thin Cook Liquid + Thick Finish Sauce)

A **6-qt Instant Pot** pulled pork designed for a game-day crowd: **dry-brined cubes** (for deeper seasoning + better sear), pressure-cooked in a **thin Cheerwine cooking liquid** (to avoid burn issues), then shredded and finished with **thickened Cackalacky Cheerwine Sweet Sauce**.

**Tags:** pork, bbq-style, instant-pot, pressure-cook, dry-brine, game-day, make-ahead, crowd-pleaser

---

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** ~8 big sandwiches or ~12 sliders (plus leftovers)
- **Active Prep:** 20–30 minutes (day-of)
- **Dry brine:** 8–24 hours (best), 1–2 hours (minimum)
- **Pressure cook:** 40 minutes High
- **Release:** 15 minutes Natural Release, then vent
- **Total day-of:** ~1 hr 30 min

---

## Equipment
- 6-qt Instant Pot (or equivalent electric pressure cooker)
- 12-inch stainless-steel skillet (for searing)
- Rimmed sheet pan + wire rack (for dry brine)
- Tongs + wooden spoon
- Instant-read thermometer (helpful, not mandatory)
- Large bowl (for shredding) + forks (or mixer)

---

## Ingredients

### Pork + dry brine
- **Boneless pork butt/shoulder:** 4–5 lb

**Kosher salt (for dry brine):**
- Target: **0.75% of meat weight** (by grams)
  - Practical guide: **14 g for 4 lb**, **17 g for 5 lb**  
  - (Weighing is best; sauce adds salt later.)

### Rub (no sugar; sweetness comes from finishing sauce)
- 2 tbsp smoked paprika  
- 1 tbsp chili powder  
- 2 tsp garlic powder  
- 2 tsp onion powder  
- 2 tsp ground cumin  
- 1½ tsp black pepper  
- 1 tsp dry mustard (optional)  
- ¼–½ tsp cayenne (optional)

### Thin cooking liquid (goes in the pot)
- 12 oz Cheerwine (1 can)  
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth (or water)  
- 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar  
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)  
- 1 tsp liquid smoke (optional)

### Aromatics
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced or quartered  
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed (optional)

### Sauce after (thick finish)
- 1 to 1½ cups **Cackalacky Cheerwine Sweet Sauce**, plus more for serving

---

## Quick Overview
- **Dry brine cubed pork** 8–24h for better seasoning and browning.
- **Sear in stainless**, then **deglaze into the Instant Pot** (free flavor, low burn risk).
- **Pressure cook with thin liquid only**; keep **thick sugary sauce for after**.
- Cook to **shred-tender**; if it doesn’t shred easily, it needs more time.
- **Shred → add some defatted juices → sauce gradually** until glossy (not soupy).

---

## Instructions

### 1) Day-before: cube + dry brine (8–24 hours)
1. Trim off only the thickest exterior fat (leave marbling).
2. Cut pork into **~2-inch cubes**.
3. Sprinkle measured **kosher salt** evenly over all cubes.
4. Arrange cubes on a wire rack over a sheet pan and refrigerate **uncovered** for **8–24 hours**.

**If short on time:** 1–2 hours still helps; overnight is best.

---

### 2) Day-of: rub + sear in stainless skillet
1. Toss pork cubes with the **rub** until evenly coated.
2. Heat a stainless skillet over **medium-high**. Add **1–2 tbsp neutral oil**.
3. Sear pork **in batches** (don’t crowd): **2–3 min per side**, aiming for browned faces (not fully cooked through).
4. Transfer seared pork to a bowl as you go.

**Notes**
- If the pan starts smoking aggressively, lower heat slightly.
- You only need **2–3 browned sides** per cube.

---

### 3) Deglaze skillet + load the Instant Pot (burn-resistant)
1. Deglaze the skillet with a small splash of the **Cheerwine** or **broth**. Scrape up all browned bits.
2. Pour the deglazing liquid (and loosened fond) into the Instant Pot.
3. Add onion (and garlic if using) to the pot.
4. Add the rest of the **thin cooking liquid**: Cheerwine + broth + vinegar (+ Worcestershire/liquid smoke if using).
5. Add pork cubes on top.

**Important:** Do **not** add Cackalacky sauce before pressure cooking.

---

### 4) Pressure cook
1. Seal lid. Cook on **High Pressure: 40 minutes**.
2. Let pressure release naturally for **15 minutes**, then vent remaining pressure.

**If it won’t shred easily**
- Pressure cook **10 more minutes** (High)
- Natural release **10 minutes**
- Repeat until it shreds with minimal resistance

---

### 5) Shred + finish (hybrid sauce step)
1. Use tongs to move pork to a large bowl (leave onions behind unless you want them mixed in).
2. Pour pot liquid into a measuring cup and **defat** (spoon fat off the top).
3. Shred pork (forks or mixer).
4. Mix in **½ to 1 cup** defatted cooking juices to keep pork juicy.
5. Add **Cackalacky Cheerwine Sweet Sauce** gradually until glossy and cohesive.
   - Start with **¾ cup**, toss, then add more as needed (typical total: **1 to 1½ cups**).

**Optional “slider-stickiness” move**
- Simmer the sauce in a small saucepan **5–8 minutes** to reduce slightly, then toss with pork.

---

### 6) Hold for the party
- Return pork to Instant Pot on **Keep Warm** with a small splash of reserved defatted juices.
- Serve extra sauce on the side.

---

## Troubleshooting
- **Too sweet:** add **1–2 tsp apple cider vinegar** (repeat to taste) + a pinch of black pepper.
- **Too loose/soupy:** lid off, **Sauté (Low)** a few minutes, stirring, until it tightens.
- **Dry:** add more reserved defatted juices and re-toss.
- **Not shredding:** it’s under-tender → **+10 min pressure** and re-check.

---

## Variations
1. **Carolina-ish tang cut:** after saucing, add **2–3 tbsp** cider vinegar + red pepper flakes.
2. **Smokier:** keep liquid smoke + add **½ tsp chipotle powder** to rub.
3. **Crispy edges:** broil shredded pork 2–4 minutes (watch closely), then re-toss with sauce.
4. **Slaw-forward sliders:** sauce slightly lighter; let slaw provide moisture + crunch.
