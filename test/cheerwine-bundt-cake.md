# Cheerwine-Soaked Bundt Cake
*Layered syrup soak + thick glaze that sets (potluck-safe)*

## Overview
A **homemade vanilla-almond Bundt** where the Cheerwine flavor comes from a **warm, layered Cheerwine syrup soak** (rum-cake technique), then finishes with a **thick glaze that sets** for clean transport and slicing.

## Tags
dessert, cake, southern, bake, potluck, make-ahead, crowd-friendly

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** 1 Bundt cake, **16–20 slices** (or **24 small party slivers**)
- **Active time:** 25–35 minutes
- **Bake:** **55–70 minutes** (start checking at 50)
- **Cool / Unmold / Soak / Glaze:** 60–120 minutes
- **Total:** ~2.5–4 hours
- **Make-ahead:** Best **after an overnight rest** (flavor + texture improve)

## Ingredients

### Cake batter
- Baking spray with flour, for the pan *(or oil/shortening + flour)*
- **3 cups** all-purpose flour
- **2 tsp** baking powder
- **1/2 tsp** baking soda
- **1 tsp** kosher salt
- **1 cup (2 sticks)** unsalted butter, room temp
- **2 cups** granulated sugar
- **1/4 cup** neutral oil *(reduced to keep crumb sturdy for soaking)*
- **4** large eggs, room temp
- **2 tsp** vanilla extract
- **1 tsp** almond extract
- **1 cup** sour cream
- **1 cup** Cheerwine, room temp *(let it go slightly flat to reduce foaming)*

### Cheerwine soak syrup (layered soak)
- **12 oz (1 bottle)** Cheerwine
- **1/2 cup** granulated sugar
- **Pinch** kosher salt
- **2 Tbsp** unsalted butter
- **1 Tbsp** lemon juice *(optional but recommended)*

> **Soak target:** apply **3/4 to 1 cup max** syrup total to the cake, in thin layers. Do not force the rest.

### Set glaze
- **2 cups** powdered sugar
- **2–4 Tbsp** reserved **butter-free** Cheerwine reduction (see Step 6)
- **1/2 tsp** vanilla extract
- **Tiny pinch** kosher salt

---

## Quick Overview
- Bake a sturdy vanilla-almond Bundt  
- Unmold on time (10–20 min)  
- Reduce Cheerwine into syrup; reserve **butter-free** portion for glaze  
- Soak **in layers** using **3/4–1 cup max** syrup total  
- Finish with a thick glaze that sets  

---

## Instructions

### 1) Prep the pan and oven
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** (middle rack).
2. Thoroughly coat Bundt pan with **baking spray-with-flour**, hitting every crevice.  
   - If using grease + flour: grease aggressively, flour, tap out excess. Do this **right before** filling.

### 2) Mix dry ingredients
3. Whisk together **flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt**.

### 3) Build batter (avoid toughness)
4. Cream **butter + sugar** on medium until lighter and fluffy, **3–5 minutes**. Scrape bowl.
5. Beat in **oil (1/4 cup)**.
6. Add **eggs one at a time**, mixing until each disappears. Add **vanilla + almond**.
7. On low speed, alternate:
   - 1/3 dry → 1/2 sour cream → 1/3 dry → 1/2 sour cream → final 1/3 dry → **Cheerwine**
8. Mix just until smooth. Stop early rather than late.

### 4) Bake
9. Pour into pan (do not exceed **3/4 full**). Tap 2–3 times to pop bubbles.
10. Bake **55–70 minutes**, **start checking at 50**.  
    - If browning early, tent loosely with foil.
11. Doneness checks:
    - Toothpick comes out clean (a few moist crumbs ok), **and**
    - Cake springs back when lightly pressed, **and**
    - Optional “no doubt”: internal temp **200–205°F** in the center (avoid touching the pan).

### 5) Unmold correctly (release timing matters)
12. Cool in pan on a rack **10–20 minutes** (aim ~15).
13. Run a thin knife/offset spatula around the **outer edge** and **center tube**.
14. Tap firmly all around the pan, then invert onto a rack.  
    - If stubborn: wrap the pan with a hot damp towel for 1–2 minutes, then try again.

### 6) Make syrup + reserve butter-free reduction + soak in layers
15. In a saucepan combine: **12 oz Cheerwine + 1/2 cup sugar + pinch salt**.
16. Simmer on medium (stir to dissolve sugar), then simmer **6–10 minutes** until slightly thickened.  
    - Practical target: reduce to about **~1 1/4 cups** total syrup (verify in a heatproof measuring cup).
17. **Reserve butter-free syrup for glaze:** pour off **1/4 cup** of this syrup into a small bowl (no butter added yet).
18. Turn off heat. Stir **2 Tbsp butter** into the remaining syrup until melted. Stir in **lemon juice** (optional).
19. Place cake on a rack over a sheet pan. Poke holes across the top and sides with a skewer.
20. **Layered soak (key technique):**
    - Brush/spoon a thin coat of syrup.
    - Wait **5–10 minutes**.
    - Repeat until you’ve used **3/4 to 1 cup total** soak syrup.
    - **Stop if it pools** or looks wet instead of glossy. Pause 10 minutes and reassess.

### 7) Make a glaze that sets
21. Whisk: **powdered sugar + 2 Tbsp reserved butter-free syrup + vanilla + tiny pinch salt**.
22. Adjust thickness:
    - Add more syrup **1 tsp at a time** until **thick but pourable**.
    - If too thin, add more powdered sugar.
23. Drizzle glaze over cake. Let set **15–30 minutes**.

## Serving & Transport
- For a party: slice **half** into thin slivers and leave half intact for fresh slicing (prevents drying).
- Best schedule: soak the cake, wrap, rest overnight; glaze the next day.

---

## Common Issues
- **Gummy pockets:** too much syrup too fast → thin layers only; give it time between coats.
- **Cheerwine still subtle:** reduce syrup a bit longer next time, or soak closer to the 1 cup max (still layered).
- **Stuck cake:** incomplete pan coverage or cooled too long in pan → release at 10–20 minutes; knife + tapping.

---

## Make-Ahead & Storage
- **Best:** soak fully, wrap, rest overnight; glaze day-of.
- **Room temp:** 2–3 days wrapped.
- **Fridge:** up to ~5 days (bring to room temp before serving).
- **Freezer:** freeze **unsoaked/unglazed** for best texture; thaw, then soak + glaze.
