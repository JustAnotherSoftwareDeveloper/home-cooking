# Mixed Greens with Pear, Cranberries, Toasted Nuts & Light Balsamic

## Overview
A crisp, not-sweet, toss-at-the-pass salad that cuts through rich brunch dishes. Mixed greens (or chopped romaine), juicy pear, tart dried cranberries, and toasted nuts—all glossed with a pale, light balsamic vinaigrette so the greens stay bright.

## Yield & Timing
- **Serves:** 10–12 (side portions)  
- **Active time:** 10–15 minutes  
- **Serve:** immediately after tossing

## Grocery List (by aisle)
- **Produce:** 2 large ripe-but-firm pears (Bosc or Bartlett); 10 oz spring mix *or* 3 romaine hearts (to chop); 1 small lemon (optional, for anti-browning); 1 small shallot (optional)
- **Pantry:** Dried cranberries (3/4 cup); white balsamic vinegar (or regular balsamic); extra-virgin olive oil; Dijon mustard; honey or maple syrup; kosher salt; black pepper
- **Nuts/Seeds:** Raw walnuts or pecans (1 cup) *or* pepitas (nut-free option)

## Equipment
Sheet pan, parchment (optional), small bowl or jar (for dressing), whisk, large salad bowl, tongs, chef’s knife & board

## Ingredients (use-order)
- **For the nuts:**  
  - 1 cup raw walnuts or pecans (or pepitas for nut-free)
- **For the dressing (yields ~3/4 cup):**  
  - 1/4 cup white balsamic vinegar *(or 3 tbsp regular balsamic + 1 tbsp water)*  
  - 1 tsp Dijon mustard  
  - 1–2 tsp honey or maple syrup (to taste)  
  - 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil  
  - 3/4 tsp kosher salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper  
  - (Optional) 1 tbsp very finely minced shallot
- **For the salad:**  
  - 10 oz spring mix *(or ~10 packed cups chopped romaine)*  
  - 2 large pears, cored and thin-sliced (1/4-inch)  
  - 3/4 cup dried cranberries

## Instructions
1. **Toast the nuts (7–10 min):** Heat oven to **350°F**. Spread nuts on a sheet pan; bake until **fragrant and lightly deepened** in color, **7–8 minutes**, stirring once. Cool 3–5 minutes, then rough-chop.
2. **Make the light balsamic vinaigrette (2–3 min):** In a small bowl, whisk vinegar, Dijon, honey, salt, and pepper (and shallot, if using). Stream in oil while whisking until lightly thickened. Taste for **bright but smooth** balance. *(Target ratio ≈ 3:1 oil:acid.)*
3. **Slice the pears (1–2 min):** Core and thin-slice. If staging a few minutes, toss slices with **1 tsp lemon juice** to slow browning; pat dry before adding.
