# Pastane-Style Revani (Metal Pan)
**Yogurt–Semolina Syrup Cake with Lemon-Peel Syrup, Overnight Set**

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** 9x9-in light-colored metal pan, ~12–16 pieces
- **Active:** ~25–35 minutes
- **Bake:** ~25–33 minutes
- **Soak/Rest:** **Overnight (8–16 hours) — required**
- **Total:** ~1.5–2 hours day-of + overnight

---

## Equipment
- **9x9-in light-colored metal pan**
- Saucepan (2+ qt) for syrup
- Electric mixer (hand or stand)
- Spatula, whisk
- Ladle or large spoon (for staged syruping)
- Vegetable peeler (for lemon peel strips)
- Optional: parchment paper

---

## Ingredients

### Syrup (make first)
- Granulated sugar: **1 1/2 cups (300 g)**
- Water: **1 1/2 cups (355 ml)**
- Lemon peel: **2 wide strips** (minimal white pith)
- Lemon juice: **2 Tbsp (30 ml)**

### Cake
- Light olive oil (or neutral oil): **4 Tbsp (60 ml)**, divided  
  - **2 Tbsp** for pan  
  - **2 Tbsp** in batter
- **Eggs: 3 large**
- Granulated sugar: **1 cup (200 g)**
- Plain whole-milk yogurt: **1 cup (225 g)**
- Fine semolina: **1 cup (165 g)**
- All-purpose flour: **3 Tbsp**
- Baking powder: **1 tsp**
- Vanilla extract: **2 tsp (10 ml)**
- Lemon juice: **2 Tbsp (30 ml)** *(replaces zest)*
- Fine salt: **pinch** *(recommended)*

### Optional garnish
- Finely chopped pistachios and/or desiccated coconut

---

## Quick Overview
- **Texture target:** Light, even crumb that slices cleanly after a full soak.
- **Syrup target:** Thin and pourable; **cool syrup + warm cake**, applied in **passes**.
- **Key technique:** Whip eggs + sugar until thick/pale; rest batter briefly; syrup slowly.
- **Rest target:** **Overnight in the fridge** for uniform absorption and “pastane” texture.

---

## Instructions

### 1) Make the lemon-peel syrup (cool completely)
1. In a saucepan, combine **sugar + water + lemon peel**.
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until sugar dissolves.
3. Reduce to a gentle simmer (low) and simmer **10–12 minutes**, uncovered, until just slightly thickened but still very pourable.
4. Add **2 Tbsp lemon juice** and simmer **2–3 minutes** more.
5. Remove from heat, **discard the peel**, and cool syrup to **room temperature**.

**Checkpoint:** Syrup should pour in a thin stream. If it feels sticky/thick as it cools, add **2–4 Tbsp water**, warm briefly to dissolve, and re-cool.

---

### 2) Preheat and prep the pan
1. Preheat oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Grease a **9x9 light-colored metal pan** with **2 Tbsp oil**.  
   - Optional: line the bottom with parchment for the cleanest release.

---

### 3) Mix the batter (bakery lift)
1. In a large bowl, whip **3 large eggs + 1 cup sugar** on high **5–6 minutes** until pale, thick, and expanded.
2. Mix in remaining **2 Tbsp oil** and **yogurt** just until smooth.
3. Add **semolina, flour, baking powder, vanilla, 2 Tbsp lemon juice, and a pinch of salt**. Mix until just combined; scrape bowl well.
4. Rest batter **10 minutes** (semolina hydrates; crumb bakes more evenly).

---

### 4) Bake (metal pan profile)
1. Pour batter into prepared pan and smooth the top.
2. Bake **25–33 minutes**, starting checks at **25 minutes**.

**Doneness cues:**
- Top is evenly golden
- Center springs back
- Toothpick comes out clean (a couple moist crumbs are fine; wet batter is not)

---

### 5) Syrup the cake (staged for even absorption)
1. Remove cake from oven and let it rest **10 minutes** (warm, not blazing hot).
2. Confirm syrup is **cool/room temp** (not warm).
3. Ladle syrup over the cake in **3–5 passes**, waiting **30–60 seconds** between passes.

**Checkpoint:** Syrup should absorb without forming a persistent puddle. If pooling happens, gently redistribute with the ladle and give it a minute—absorption should catch up.

---

### 6) Overnight rest (required) and serve
1. Let the syruped cake sit uncovered **20–30 minutes** to vent steam.
2. Cover tightly and refrigerate **8–16 hours**.
3. Before serving, bring to room temp **45–60 minutes**.
4. Slice into diamonds or squares; garnish if desired.

---

## Storage
- **Refrigerated:** 3–4 days, tightly covered.
- **Serving temp:** best at room temp (reheating tends to soften structure and can make it feel “syrupy”).

---

## Common Failure Modes
- **Soggy bottom / dry top:** cake too thick or syrup dumped too fast → use the 9x9 pan and staged syruping.
- **Gummy texture:** syrup too warm or cake too hot → fully cool syrup; rest cake 10 minutes before syruping.
- **Dry even after syrup:** overbaked → pull as soon as toothpick is clean and top is golden.
