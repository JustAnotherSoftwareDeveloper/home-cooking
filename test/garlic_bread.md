
# Roasted-Garlic & Fontina Stuffed Cheesy Bread (Folded Slab)

## Overview
Domino’s-style cheesy bread, but elevated: cold-fermented NY-ish dough, stuffed with **young Fontina + low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella**, folded, slashed to vent, and baked hot on a metal sheet (or on a preheated baking steel for extra bottom browning). Finished with silky roasted-garlic butter and a Parm shower.

---

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** 10–14 appetizer strips (8–10 hearty side portions)
- **Active:** ~35–45 minutes (not counting dough/garlic make-ahead)
- **Total:**  
  - **Cold-ferment (best):** 24–72 hours, mostly unattended  
  - **Same-day:** ~3–4 hours with a warm rise

## Equipment
Half-sheet **metal** pan + parchment; large bowl; small baking dish or foil (for garlic); serrated knife; pastry brush; (optional) **baking steel/stone** on a lower rack.

---

## Ingredients (in use-order)

### Quick NY-ish Dough (makes 1 slab; ~18 oz dough)
- 2½ cups bread flour  
- ¾ cup warm water, plus 1–2 Tbsp if needed  
- 1 tsp instant yeast  
- 1 Tbsp sugar  
- 1½ tsp kosher salt  
- 1 Tbsp olive oil

### Roasted-Garlic Herb Butter
- 2 heads garlic, tops trimmed  
- 2 tsp olive oil  
- 8 Tbsp (1 stick) unsalted butter  
- 2 Tbsp finely chopped parsley or chives  
- ½ tsp kosher salt  
- Pinch garlic powder + red pepper (optional)

### Cheese & Finish
- 6 oz Fontina, shredded  
- 8 oz low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, shredded  
- ½ cup finely grated Parmesan or Pecorino, divided

---

## Instructions

### A) Dough (cold-ferment or same-day)
1. **Mix (5 min):** Whisk flour, yeast, sugar, and salt. Add ¾ cup warm water and oil; stir to a tacky dough. If dry patches remain, add up to 2 Tbsp more water.  
2. **Knead (3–5 min):** Knead until smooth and elastic.  
3. **Ferment—choose one:**  
   - **Cold-ferment (best):** Lightly oil bowl; cover and refrigerate **24–72 hours**. Pull out **60–90 min** before baking.  
   - **Same-day:** Cover and rise warm until doubled, **1½–2½ hours**.

### B) Roast the garlic (can do 1–3 days ahead)
4. **Roast (45–60 min):** Heat oven to **375°F**. Trim ~⅛–¼″ from each head to expose cloves; drizzle with oil; wrap tightly in foil (or lidded dish). Roast until very soft and **pale-gold** (not dark brown). Cool.  
5. **Make butter:** Squeeze cloves into a small pan; add butter. Warm **low** just until melted and fragrant (do **not** brown). Stir in herbs, salt, and optional spices. Keep warm.

> **Food safety:** Garlic-in-butter/oil must be **refrigerated** and used within ~4 days (or **frozen**). Do not store at room temp.

### C) Shape, Fill, Fold
6. **Preheat:** Place a rack center; if using a **baking steel/stone**, put it on a lower rack and preheat to **450°F** for 30–45 minutes.  
7. **Prep pan:** Line a metal half-sheet pan with parchment.  
8. **Roll:** On a lightly oiled surface, roll dough to a **10×14-inch** rectangle (about ⅛–¼″ thick).  
9. **Layer cheese:** Scatter **mozzarella + Fontina** over **one half**, leaving a ½″ border. Drizzle/brush a few spoonfuls of **roasted-garlic butter** over the cheese (reserve most for finishing).  
10. **Fold & seal:** Fold the bare half over the filling; press edges to seal well. Transfer to the pan.  
11. **Slash & top:** Brush top lightly with garlic butter; sprinkle **2–3 Tbsp Parm**. Make **5–6 shallow diagonal slashes** across the top to vent.

### D) Bake & Finish
12. **Bake:** **450°F for 12–16 minutes** until deep golden and the seams sizzle. For maximum bottom color, set the pan **directly on the preheated steel/stone** for the first 8–10 minutes.  
13. **Finish:** Brush hot bread generously with the **remaining roasted-garlic butter**; shower with the rest of the **Parm**. Return to oven **1–2 minutes** to set the cheese.  
14. **Rest & slice:** Cool **5 minutes**, then cut into **1–1½″ strips** with a serrated knife. Serve hot with warm marinara.

## Troubleshooting
- **Greasy pockets:** Pull as soon as cheese fully melts; overheating squeezes out fat.  
- **Soggy/underdone bottom:** Use a **metal pan** and, if possible, bake on a **preheated steel/stone**.  
- **Cheese leaks:** Seal edges firmly and keep slashes shallow.  
- **Harsh/bitter garlic:** Keep cloves pale and butter unbrowned; gentle heat only.  
- **Too pale:** Add 1–3 minutes or move to a higher rack for the last minutes.

---

## Make-Ahead & Holding
- **Dough:** Cold-ferment **24–72 h** in the fridge for best flavor.  
- **Garlic butter:** Make **up to 4 days** ahead; refrigerate (or freeze). Warm gently to liquefy before finishing.  
- **Game-day hold:** Keep sliced pieces on a rack-lined sheet at **200°F** for up to 30–45 minutes; re-brush with a little butter before serving.

---

## Notes & Variations
- **Herb profile:** Add a pinch of dried oregano to the butter for stronger “pizzeria” aroma.  
- **Heat option:** Finish with a drizzle of Calabrian chile oil.  
- **Cheese swap:** Scamorza (smoked mozzarella) blends beautifully with Fontina for a subtle smoke note.  
- **Size tweak:** For thinner bread, roll to 11×15″ and start checking at 10–12 minutes.
