# Apple–Cinnamon Protein Baked Oatmeal Bars (Greek Yogurt + Eggs)

## Title & Overview
Protein-forward baked oatmeal bars with warm apple-cinnamon flavor and a clean, sliceable crumb. Greek yogurt and eggs boost protein and help the slab set; oats and diced apples add chew. Designed for weekly prep with leftovers, simple reheating, and freezer-friendliness. A brief 10-minute rest before baking improves structure and reduces crumble. [1]

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: 9 to 12 bars (9 in x 9 in pan), with leftovers
- Active Prep: 15 minutes
- Inactive Prep: 45 to 60 minutes (cooling and slicing)
- Cook: 32 to 38 minutes (see foil-tent cue) [1]
- Total: ~1 hour 30 minutes
- Make-ahead: Refrigerate within 2 hours; good 3 to 4 days refrigerated or freeze for longer quality (see Storage). [3]

## Ingredients
- 2 3/4 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 1 1/2 cups milk (dairy or unsweetened almond)
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt (2 percent or whole)
- 2 large eggs
- 1 large egg white (or use a 3rd whole egg)
- 1/3 cup honey (or maple syrup)
- 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 medium apple, cored and finely diced (about 1 1/2 cups) [pre-prep]
- Optional add-ins (pick any, keep total modest):
  - 3 Medjool dates, finely chopped (about 1/4 cup loose) [pre-prep]
  - 1/2 cup walnuts or pecans, toasted and cooled, chopped [pre-prep]
  - 3 Tbsp hemp hearts

## Equipment
- 9 in x 9 in metal or ceramic baking pan
- Parchment paper and avocado oil spray
- 2 mixing bowls, whisk, spatula
- Knife and cutting board

## Detailed Instructions
1) Prep: Heat oven to 350 deg F. Line a 9 in x 9 in pan with a parchment sling and lightly oil it.
2) Dry bowl: Whisk oats, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl.
3) Wet bowl: Whisk milk, Greek yogurt, eggs, egg white, honey, applesauce, and vanilla until smooth.
4) Combine: Pour wet into dry and stir until no dry pockets remain. Fold in diced apple and any optional add-ins (dates, nuts, hemp). **Rest 10 minutes** so oats hydrate; this improves set and cleaner slices. [1]
5) Bake: Spread evenly and tap the pan to level. **Bake 32 to 38 minutes**, tenting with foil after ~20 minutes if the top is browning quickly. Pull when the edges are golden and the center is set (a toothpick near center comes out mostly clean). [1]
6) Cool and slice: Cool 15 minutes in the pan, lift out by the parchment, then cool 30 to 45 minutes more before slicing into 9 (large) or 12 (smaller) bars.

## Nutrition Snapshot (qualitative)
- Protein-forward from Greek yogurt and eggs; optional hemp hearts add more.
- Oats and apples provide fiber; sweetness is tuned with honey/maple and a small amount of dates (optional).
- Sodium stays modest by salting lightly and skipping processed add-ins.

## Special Notes & Variations
- Storage & Reheat: Refrigerate within 2 hours; eat within **3 to 4 days** or freeze for longer quality. Reheat single bars at **350 deg F for 5 to 10 minutes** or **about 60 seconds in the microwave**; warm a covered whole pan at **350 deg F for ~15 to 20 minutes**. [2][3]
- Freezer guidance: Freeze up to about 3 to 4 months for best quality. Reheat from frozen at 325 to 350 deg F for 10 to 15 minutes, or microwave briefly before toasting. [3]
- Serving ideas: Dollop with Greek yogurt; drizzle with a little honey; add berries; or serve warm with a splash of milk.
- Dietary toggles:
  - Dairy-free: use plain soy or coconut yogurt and plant milk; keep the eggs for structure.
  - Lower-sugar: reduce honey to 1/4 cup and skip dates; lean on spices and nuts for interest.
  - Nut-free: skip nuts; use seeds (hemp, pepitas, sunflower).
