# Spicy Korean Beef Companion for the Base Recipe

Scaled for the full **5-cup dry-rice batch** and approximately **14 cups of beef, carrots, and green beans** in the base recipe.

## Korean-Seasoned Rice

### Add before pressure cooking

* 5 cups unsalted beef stock, replacing the water
* 2 tbsp avocado oil, replacing the ghee
* 2 tbsp hot gochugaru
* 1 1/2 tsp ground ginger
* 1 tsp ground black pepper
* 6 g fine salt, approximately 1 tsp

Cook using the base recipe's existing pressure time and release method.

### Add after oven-drying

* 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
* 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Fold the sesame oil and seeds into the rice after the brief oven-drying step, immediately before portioning.

## Shortcut Spicy Bulgogi Sauce

* 1 full 16.9 oz bottle Bibigo KBBQ Sauce Original
* 1/3 cup gochujang
* 1/3 cup hot gochugaru
* 1/2 cup unsalted beef cooking liquid or water
* 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
* 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

### Instructions

1. **Whisk** the bottled sauce, gochujang, gochugaru, and cooking liquid together in a small pot.
2. **Simmer over medium-low heat for 5 to 7 minutes**, whisking frequently, until the gochugaru is hydrated and the sauce is glossy but still pourable.
3. Remove from the heat and stir in the toasted sesame oil and sesame seeds.
4. **Fold** the entire sauce batch into the finished beef, carrots, and green beans.
5. Taste the sauced mixture together with an equal amount of rice before adjusting.

   * If too thick, add unsalted beef liquid 2 tbsp at a time.
   * If not hot enough, add another 1 to 2 tbsp hot gochugaru and simmer for 2 minutes.

## Base-Recipe Salt Changes

Because the bottled sauce, gochujang, and beef stock contribute sodium, change the base seasoning to:

* Rice: **6 g fine salt**
* Beef: **2 g fine salt**
* Carrots: **no added salt**
* Green beans: **no added salt**

## Heat Level

The sauce contains approximately **1 1/8 tsp hot gochugaru per meal**, before accounting for the additional gochugaru in the rice and the heat from the gochujang.

This should produce a genuinely hot finished bowl rather than ordinary sweet bulgogi with a mild chile note.

## Ingredient Note

Bibigo KBBQ Sauce Original contains onion, garlic, wheat, soy, and sesame. This shortcut is therefore not low-FODMAP or gluten-free.
