# Thick + Spicy “Cackalacky-Style” Cheerwine BBQ Sauce (6-cup batch)

## Yield & Timing Targets
- **Target yield:** ~6 cups (48 fl oz)
- **Total cook time target:** **55–75 minutes**
  - Cheerwine reduction: **25–40 min**
  - Sauce simmer after mixing: **15–30 min**
  - Mix/whisk steps: **~5–10 min**
- **Rest:** 10–15 min to cool + re-whisk (overnight rest is best)

## Equipment
- **4–6 qt wide saucepan or Dutch oven** (wide = faster/more predictable reduction)
- Whisk + silicone spatula
- Large liquid measuring cup (8+ cups) for volume check (recommended)

## Ingredients

### Cheerwine Reduction
- **Cheerwine:** **36 fl oz** (3 × 12 fl oz cans, or measure from bottles)

### Base
- Ketchup: **4 1/2 cups**
- Tomato paste: **6 Tbsp**
- Granulated sugar: **9 Tbsp** (½ cup + 1 Tbsp)
- Light brown sugar: **6 Tbsp**
- Worcestershire sauce: **3 Tbsp**
- Distilled white vinegar: **6 Tbsp**
- Apple cider vinegar: **3 Tbsp**
- Liquid smoke: **1 1/2 tsp**

### Spices (Hotter Profile)
- Smoked paprika: **1 Tbsp**
- Garlic powder: **1 1/2 tsp**
- Onion powder: **1 1/2 tsp**
- Dry mustard: **1 1/2 tsp**
- Black pepper: **2 Tbsp**
- Chipotle powder: **1 Tbsp**
- Cayenne: **2 1/4 tsp**
- Crushed red pepper flakes: **1 1/2 tsp**
- Kosher salt: **2 1/4 tsp**, then adjust

### Optional Heat Dial
- Vinegar-forward hot sauce: **3–6 Tbsp**, to taste
- Habanero powder: up to **3/8 tsp** (only if you want it aggressively hot)

## Instructions (with time targets)

### 1) Reduce the Cheerwine (turn soda into syrup)
1. Add **36 fl oz Cheerwine** to a **wide** pot.
2. Bring to a steady simmer over **medium** (not a raging boil).  
   - **Time to reach simmer:** ~**4–8 minutes**
3. Simmer until reduced to **~8 fl oz (1 cup)**.  
   - **Target time:** **25–40 minutes**
   - **Checkpoint cues:**
     - Bubbles get tighter/smaller and look “syrupy”
     - Smells like cherry syrup, not soda
     - Volume looks close to **1 cup**

### 2) Build the sauce
4. Lower heat to **low**.
5. Whisk in **ketchup + tomato paste** until fully smooth.  
   - **Time:** **2–4 minutes**
6. Whisk in sugars, Worcestershire, vinegars, liquid smoke, and all spices.  
   - **Time:** **1–2 minutes**

### 3) Simmer to thickness and final yield
7. Raise to a **gentle simmer** over **medium-low**.  
   - **Time:** **3–6 minutes**
8. Simmer **uncovered**, stirring every 2–3 minutes and scraping the bottom, until you reach **~6 cups** and the sauce is glossy/thick.  
   - **Target time:** **15–30 minutes**
   - **Texture checkpoints (more important than minutes):**
     - A spatula drag leaves a trail that holds **~2 seconds** before slowly closing
     - Sauce sheets off a spoon slowly (not a thin stream)

### 4) Finish + adjust
9. Off heat, rest **10–15 minutes**, then whisk again.
10. Adjust in this order (small steps):
   - **Salt** (pinch at a time)
   - **Vinegar** (½ tsp at a time if too sweet/heavy)
   - **Heat** (hot sauce 1 Tbsp at a time, or cayenne ¼ tsp at a time)

## Hitting 6 Cups Exactly (recommended)
- When it *looks right*, pour into a large measuring cup and confirm **48 fl oz**.
- If you’re at **6.25–6.5 cups**, return to pot and simmer **another 5–10 minutes**.

## Notes / Guardrails
- Thicker sauces concentrate **sweetness and smoke** quickly—stick with **1 1/2 tsp** liquid smoke unless you know you want more.
- If the sauce gets **too thick**, whisk in **1 Tbsp water at a time** off heat until pourable.
- If it gets **too smoky**, dilute with **2 Tbsp ketchup + 1 Tbsp water**, then re-balance with a small splash of vinegar.
