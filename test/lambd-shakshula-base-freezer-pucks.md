# Lamb Shakshuka Base Freezer Pucks (No Eggs) for Rice Bowls

## Title & Overview
These are 1-cup freezer pucks of a thick, reduced lamb shakshuka-style sauce: browned ground lamb cooked with onion, peppers, garlic, warm spices, tomato paste, and tomatoes until spoon-coating. The base is designed to be frozen **without eggs** (eggs are better added fresh, not frozen into the sauce). [1](#ref-1) The sauce is intentionally reduced so it reheats in the microwave without turning your rice watery.

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: 10 x 1-cup freezer pucks (about 10 cups total; leftovers-by-design)
- Active Prep: 20 to 25 minutes
- Inactive Prep: 20 to 30 minutes (simmer/reduce) + cooling time
- Cook: 30 to 40 minutes
- Total: About 1 hour 30 minutes (including cooling enough to portion)
- Make-ahead: Refrigerate within 2 hours after cooking. [3](#ref-3) Keep refrigerated 3 to 4 days, or freeze; reheat to 165 deg F. [3](#ref-3)[4](#ref-4)

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped [pre-prep]
- 2 bell peppers, finely chopped [pre-prep]
- 1 jalapeno, finely chopped (optional) [pre-prep]
- 5 cloves garlic, minced [pre-prep]
- 2 tsp ground cumin [pre-prep]
- 2 tsp sweet paprika [pre-prep]
- 1/2 tsp ground turmeric (optional) [pre-prep]
- 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon (optional) [pre-prep]
- 2 lb ground lamb
- 3 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 (14.5 oz) cans diced tomatoes
- Kosher salt:
  - 1 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal (about 4.5 g), or
  - 1 1/2 tsp Morton kosher salt (about 7.5 g)
- Black pepper, to taste
- Red pepper flakes, to taste (optional)

Optional for serving (do not freeze in the puck)
- Lemon wedges
- Plain Greek yogurt
- Chopped mint or parsley

## Detailed Instructions

### A) Set up and mise en place
1. Stage your freezer molds first. Put ten 1-cup silicone molds on a rimmed sheet pan that fits flat in your freezer (prevents spills when you move filled molds).
2. Set out tools: wooden spoon or spatula, ladle, 1-cup measuring cup, and 1 to 2 shallow containers for cooling.
3. Prep vegetables for even cooking:
   - Chop onion and peppers small (about 1/4 inch) for a tighter puck that mixes into rice more evenly.
   - Mince garlic; keep it separate so it does not burn.

### B) Build the base flavor

4. Heat the pot correctly. Use a wide 5 to 6 qt pot or Dutch oven. Heat avocado oil over medium-high until it shimmers.
5. Sweat then lightly brown onion and peppers:
   - Add onion and bell peppers (and jalapeno if using).
   - Stir to coat, then let sit 60 to 90 seconds between stirs so edges lightly brown.
   - Cook 6 to 10 minutes total, until softened and slightly sweet.
6. Bloom spices in the vegetable fat:
   - Reduce heat to medium.
   - Add cumin, paprika, turmeric (optional), and cinnamon (optional).
   - Stir constantly 20 to 40 seconds until toasted-smelling and glossy.
7. Add garlic briefly:
   - Add minced garlic and stir 20 to 30 seconds, just until fragrant.

### C) Brown the lamb and control fat

8. Add lamb and break it up immediately:
   - Add ground lamb and break into small crumbles (pea-size to marble-size).
   - Spread it out to contact the hot pan (helps browning vs steaming).
9. Brown well for depth:
   - Cook 8 to 12 minutes, stirring and scraping browned bits from the bottom.
   - Continue until you see real browning and most liquid has cooked off.
   - Ground lamb should reach 160 deg F. [5](#ref-5)
10. Degrease if needed:
   - If a clear fat layer pools, spoon off some (leave 1 to 2 tbsp behind).
   - Do not remove all fat; a little keeps the sauce rich after reheating.

### D) Tomato build and "puck thickness" reduction

11. Cook the tomato paste:
   - Add tomato paste and stir into lamb and vegetables.
   - Cook 60 to 90 seconds until slightly darker and no longer smells raw.
12. Add tomatoes and bring to a steady simmer:
   - Add crushed tomatoes and both cans of diced tomatoes.
   - Stir thoroughly, scraping the bottom to dissolve any fond.
   - Bring to a steady simmer, then reduce heat to maintain gentle constant bubbling.
13. Season early, adjust late:
   - Add kosher salt and several grinds of black pepper.
   - Add red pepper flakes if desired (you can add more after reheating).
14. Reduce uncovered until scoopable and cohesive:
   - Simmer uncovered 20 to 30 minutes.
   - Stir every 2 to 3 minutes at first; every 30 to 60 seconds near the end to prevent scorching.
   - Done when:
     - mixture mounds on a spoon (not soupy), and
     - a spatula trail closes slowly.
15. Yield sanity check (no dilution):
   - If >10 cups, portion 10 pucks and refrigerate remainder as a bonus serving.
   - If short, do not add water; stop reducing sooner next time, or add 1 to 2 tbsp tomato paste and simmer 3 to 5 minutes to thicken.

### E) Cool, portion, and freeze

16. Cool quickly and safely. Spread sauce into shallow containers so it cools faster, then refrigerate within 2 hours. [3](#ref-3)
17. Portion precisely:
   - Once fully cool, stir to redistribute fat and solids.
   - Fill each mold with exactly 1 cup, pressing down lightly to remove air pockets.
   - Smooth tops so pucks freeze flat.
18. Freeze solid, then bag and label:
   - Freeze until fully solid (typically overnight).
   - Pop out pucks and store in labeled freezer bags/containers.
   - Label with name, date, and "Reheat to 165 deg F".

### F) Reheating instructions (microwave + rice)
USDA microwave guidance emphasizes covering, stirring/rotating, standing time, and heating to 165 deg F. [4](#ref-4)

19. From frozen (recommended):
   - Put 1 frozen puck in a microwave-safe bowl (tall-sided reduces splatter).
   - Cover (lid slightly ajar or vented wrap).
   - Microwave on HIGH 3 to 4 minutes.
   - Stir and break up thoroughly; move colder center pieces to the outside.
   - Microwave 1 to 3 minutes more until steaming hot throughout.
   - Stand 1 minute, then verify 165 deg F if using a thermometer. [4](#ref-4)
20. From thawed (faster):
   - Thaw overnight in the fridge.
   - Microwave covered 2 to 3 minutes total, stirring once mid-way, until 165 deg F. [4](#ref-4)
21. Combine with rice:
   - Use hot cooked rice (fresh or reheated).
   - Fold fully hot sauce into rice immediately.
   - If you want it looser for mixing, add 1 to 2 tbsp water after the first microwave interval, then finish heating to 165 deg F before mixing.

## Special Notes & Variations

- Storage & Reheat: Refrigerate within 2 hours; keep refrigerated 3 to 4 days, or freeze. [3](#ref-3) Reheat covered, stir/rotate, stand briefly, and heat to 165 deg F. [4](#ref-4)
- Serving ideas: Mix into rice; finish with lemon, yogurt, and herbs. Add toasted pine nuts at serve time for crunch.
- Dietary toggles:
  - Lower heat: omit jalapeno and red pepper flakes; use only sweet paprika.
  - Lower fat: spoon off more rendered lamb fat after browning; leave a little behind for texture.
  - Higher veg: add 1 more bell pepper (chopped) and reduce back to the same thickness.
