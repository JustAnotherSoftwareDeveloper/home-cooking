# Lamb Shakshuka Base Freezer Pucks for Rice Bowls

## Title & Overview
These are 1-cup freezer pucks of a thick, reduced lamb shakshuka-style sauce: browned ground lamb, lots of peppers and onion, doubled garlic, and a hot baharat-inspired spice blend, simmered with tomatoes until spoon-coating and rice-ready. The lamb is browned first to build fond and deeper meat flavor. [1] The spice profile is intentionally bold so it reads well after freezing and microwaving. [2]

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: About 10 x 1-cup freezer pucks (you may end up slightly over/under depending on reduction)
- Active Prep: 20 to 25 minutes
- Inactive Prep: 20 to 30 minutes (simmer/reduce) + cooling time
- Cook: 30 to 40 minutes
- Total: About 1 hour 30 minutes (including cooling enough to portion)
- Make-ahead: Refrigerate within 2 hours after cooking. [4] Freeze for longer storage; reheat leftovers/pucks to 165 deg F. [4]

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) avocado oil
- 2 lb (900 g) ground lamb
- 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped [pre-prep]
- 4 bell peppers, finely chopped [pre-prep]
- 1 jalapeno, finely chopped (optional) [pre-prep]
- 10 cloves garlic, minced, divided (6 now, 4 later) [pre-prep]
- Hot baharat-inspired spice blend (mix in a small bowl) [pre-prep] [2]
  - 1 1/2 tbsp (9 g) ground cumin [pre-prep]
  - 1 1/2 tbsp (9 g) ground coriander [pre-prep]
  - 2 tbsp (12 g) smoked paprika [pre-prep]
  - 1 tbsp (6 g) sweet paprika [pre-prep]
  - 2 tsp (4 g) ground turmeric [pre-prep]
  - 2 tsp (4 g) black pepper [pre-prep]
  - 1 1/2 tsp (3 g) ground allspice [pre-prep]
  - 1 tsp (2 g) ground cinnamon [pre-prep]
  - 1/2 tsp (1 g) ground cardamom [pre-prep]
  - 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) ground cloves [pre-prep]
  - 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) ground nutmeg [pre-prep]
  - 1 tbsp (6 g) ground sumac (optional, recommended) [pre-prep]
  - 2 tbsp (12 g) Aleppo pepper flakes [pre-prep]
  - 1 1/2 tsp (3 g) cayenne pepper [pre-prep]
  - If you do not have Aleppo pepper: use 2 tsp crushed red pepper + 2 tsp sweet paprika (approximation).
- 3 tbsp (45 g) tomato paste
- 1 (28 oz / 794 g) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 (14.5 oz / 411 g) cans diced tomatoes
- Kosher salt:
  - 1 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal (about 4.5 g), or
  - 1 1/2 tsp Morton kosher salt (about 7.5 g)

Optional for serving (do not freeze in the puck)
- Lemon wedges [pre-prep]
- Plain Greek yogurt
- Chopped mint or parsley [pre-prep]

## Detailed Instructions
1) Stage your freezer molds first. Put ten 1-cup silicone molds on a rimmed sheet pan that fits flat in your freezer.

2) Heat the pot correctly. Use a wide 5 to 6 qt pot or Dutch oven. Heat avocado oil over medium-high until it shimmers.

3) Brown lamb first (build fond). Add ground lamb and press into a thin layer. Let it sear 2 to 3 minutes before breaking it up. Continue 6 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until well-browned and most liquid cooks off. Ground lamb should reach 160 deg F. [5]

4) Degrease if needed. If a clear fat layer pools, spoon off some (leave 1 to 2 tbsp behind). Leave browned bits in the pot.

5) Cook vegetables after lamb. Add onion, all 4 bell peppers, and jalapeno (if using). Cook 6 to 10 minutes, stirring and letting the veg sit briefly between stirs so edges lightly brown, until softened and slightly sweet.

6) Bloom spices (major flavor boost). Reduce heat to medium. Add the pre-mixed spice blend and stir constantly 20 to 40 seconds until fragrant and glossy (do not scorch). Blooming ground spices in fat intensifies and distributes flavor. [3]

7) Add garlic (part 1). Add 6 cloves minced garlic; stir 20 to 30 seconds until fragrant.

8) Return lamb. Add browned lamb (and any juices) back to the pot; stir to combine.

9) Tomato paste + garlic (part 2). Add tomato paste and cook 60 to 90 seconds, stirring, until darker and no longer smells raw. In the last 15 to 20 seconds, add the remaining 4 cloves garlic and stir until fragrant.

10) Add tomatoes and simmer. Add crushed tomatoes and diced tomatoes. Scrape the bottom to release fond. Bring to a steady simmer, then reduce heat to maintain gentle bubbling.

11) Salt, then reduce to "puck thickness." Add the kosher salt listed. Simmer uncovered 20 to 30 minutes, stirring more often near the end to prevent scorching, until the mixture mounds on a spoon (not soupy) and a spatula trail closes slowly.

12) Cool safely. Spread sauce into shallow containers to cool faster, and refrigerate within 2 hours. [4]

13) Portion into molds. Once fully cool, stir well to redistribute fat and solids. Fill each 1-cup mold with exactly 1 cup sauce, pressing gently to remove air pockets.

14) Freeze solid, then bag and label. Freeze molds until solid (typically overnight). Pop out pucks and store in labeled freezer bags with the date and "Reheat to 165 deg F." [4]

15) Reheat (microwave) and serve with rice. From frozen, microwave covered, stir and break up halfway through, then continue until steaming hot throughout and 165 deg F. [4] Fold into hot cooked rice.

- Make-Ahead Map: Steps 1-2 (prep) up to 2 days ahead; Steps 3-12 cook/cool in one session; Steps 13-14 portion and freeze for later meals; Step 15 at serving time.

## Special Notes & Variations
- Storage & Reheat: Refrigerate within 2 hours; reheat leftovers/pucks to 165 deg F. [4]
- Serving ideas: Rice bowl with lemon, yogurt, and herbs; add toasted pine nuts at serve time for crunch.
- Dietary toggles:
  - Lower heat: omit jalapeno; cut cayenne to 1/2 tsp; cut Aleppo to 1 tbsp (or omit).
  - Lower sodium: use no-salt-added tomatoes; increase lemon at serving; salt to taste after reheating.
  - Low-FODMAP leaning: replace onion with scallion greens; swap minced garlic for garlic-infused oil + chives at serving (note: flavor changes).
