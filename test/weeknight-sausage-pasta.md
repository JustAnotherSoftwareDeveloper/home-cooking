# Beans and Greens "Sausage" Pasta (Sausage-Spiced Ground Pork)

## Title & Overview
This is an Italian-ish weeknight pasta built around the classic combo of "sausage," white beans, and greens, tuned to be lighter by pushing the beans/greens volume and keeping cheese modest. [1][2]
Instead of buying sausage, you season ground pork with a fennel-forward spice blend so you control salt and heat while still getting the familiar Italian-sausage profile. [3]
The sauce turns creamy without cream by mashing some beans and finishing the pasta in the pan with starchy pasta water plus a small amount of grated cheese. [2]
It is make-ahead friendly because you can pre-season the pork and prep the produce, then cook everything quickly in one skillet. [1]

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: 6 servings (designed to leave leftovers)
- Active Prep: 20 minutes
- Inactive Prep: 10 minutes
- Cook: 25 minutes
- Total: 55 minutes
- Make-ahead: Season the pork up to 24 hours ahead (refrigerated). Chop onion/garlic and wash/chop greens up to 3 days ahead. Leftovers: refrigerate up to 3 to 4 days and reheat to 165 deg F; freeze for best quality within about 2 to 3 months. [5]

## Ingredients
- Ground pork, 1 lb [pre-prep]
- Fennel seed, 1 tbsp (toast and lightly crush) [pre-prep]
- Dried marjoram, 1 tsp [pre-prep]
- Black pepper, 1/2 tsp [pre-prep]
- Kosher salt, 1 tsp (5 g) [pre-prep]
- Garlic, 1 tsp grated (for seasoning the pork) [pre-prep]
- Optional "hot": crushed red pepper flakes, 1 to 2 tsp (or up to 1 tbsp for very hot) [pre-prep]
- Optional "hot": cayenne, 1/8 tsp [pre-prep]
- Optional "sweet": sugar, 1 tsp [pre-prep]

- Short pasta, 12 oz
- Avocado oil, 1 tbsp

- Yellow onion, 1 medium, diced [pre-prep]
- Garlic, 4 to 6 cloves, thin-sliced or minced [pre-prep]
- Tomato paste, 1 tbsp
- Optional: dry white wine, 1/4 cup
- Low-sodium broth (or water), 3/4 cup, plus more as needed

- Cannellini beans, 2 cans, drained and rinsed [pre-prep]
- Escarole, 10 to 12 oz, chopped (or kale 8 oz, ribs removed, chopped) [pre-prep]

- Parmesan or Pecorino Romano, 1/2 cup finely grated (about 2 oz), divided [pre-prep]
- Optional: lemon juice, 1 to 2 tsp [pre-prep]

## Detailed Instructions
1) Build the sausage-spice profile (5 minutes).
   - Toast and crush fennel:
     - Put fennel seeds in a dry skillet over medium heat for 60 to 90 seconds until fragrant (do not scorch).
     - Crush lightly (mortar/pestle, or a bag + rolling pin).
   - Season the pork:
     - In a bowl, combine pork, crushed fennel, marjoram, black pepper, salt, and 1 tsp grated garlic.
     - Optional hot: add red pepper flakes and cayenne.
     - Optional sweet: add sugar.
     - Mix just until evenly combined (do not knead). [3]
   - Rest 10 minutes (or cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours). [3]

2) Start the pasta water (5 minutes, mostly unattended).
   - Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
   - Salt the water lightly; you will get salt from the pork mix and the cheese.

3) Brown the sausage-spiced pork (8 to 10 minutes).
   - Heat a large, high-sided skillet over medium-high heat.
   - Add 1 tbsp avocado oil, then add the pork in large chunks.
   - Let it sit 2 to 3 minutes to brown, then break it up and keep cooking until no pink remains.
   - Food-safety check: ground pork should reach 160 deg F in the thickest part. [4]
   - Transfer pork to a bowl with a slotted spoon.
   - "Healthier dial": spoon off excess rendered fat, leaving about 1 tbsp in the pan.

4) Build the base (7 to 10 minutes).
   - Add onion; cook 3 to 4 minutes until softened.
   - Add remaining garlic; cook 30 to 60 seconds until fragrant.
   - Add tomato paste; cook 60 to 90 seconds until it darkens slightly and looks glossy.
   - Optional: deglaze with wine; simmer 30 to 60 seconds.
   - Add beans and 3/4 cup broth (or water); stir and scrape up browned bits.
   - Mash about 1/2 cup of beans against the side of the pan to create a lightly creamy base.

5) Add greens and recombine (3 to 6 minutes).
   - Escarole path:
     - Add escarole in handfuls, stirring until wilted (about 2 minutes).
   - Kale path:
     - Add kale with an extra splash of broth/water.
     - Cover 2 to 4 minutes to steam-tender, then uncover and stir.
   - Return browned pork (and any juices) to the pan; simmer 1 to 2 minutes.

6) Cook pasta and finish in the pan (5 to 7 minutes).
   - Cook pasta until 1 minute shy of al dente.
   - Reserve 1 1/2 cups pasta water, then drain.
   - Add pasta to the skillet.
   - Add 1/2 cup pasta water + 1/3 cup grated cheese.
   - Toss vigorously 60 to 90 seconds until the sauce turns glossy and clings to the pasta (add more pasta water a few tablespoons at a time if needed). [2]
   - Taste and adjust:
     - If it tastes flat: add black pepper and a pinch of salt (go slowly).
     - If it feels heavy: add a small squeeze of lemon (optional).

7) Serve.
   - Serve with remaining cheese at the table (keeps total dairy and sodium easier to control).

- Make-Ahead Map: Step 1 (season pork) up to 24 hours ahead; Step 4 prep (chop onion/garlic/greens, drain beans, grate cheese) up to 3 days ahead. Leftovers keep 3 to 4 days refrigerated and should be reheated to 165 deg F. [5]

## Special Notes & Variations
- Storage & Reheat: Refrigerate leftovers up to 3 to 4 days. Reheat to 165 deg F; in a microwave, cover and stir/rotate for more even heating. [5]
- Serving ideas:
  - Add a quick side salad with vinaigrette (acid cuts richness).
  - Add extra beans (warmed) if you want higher fiber with the same pasta amount.
- Dietary toggles:
  - Low-sodium: reduce pork salt to 3/4 tsp (about 4 g), use no-salt-added beans/broth if available, and keep cheese closer to 1/3 cup total. Increase fennel, black pepper, and lemon for flavor. [3]
  - High-protein: increase pork to 1 1/4 lb; keep pasta at 12 oz.
  - Low-FODMAP-ish: replace garlic with garlic-infused oil; replace onion with scallion green tops; choose the greens you tolerate best.
  - Gluten-free: use GF short pasta; keep the bean-mash step for sauce body.
  - Dairy-free: skip cheese; rely on bean mash + pasta water; finish with lemon and extra black pepper.
