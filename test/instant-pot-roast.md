# Red-Wine Pot Roast (Instant Pot, IP-Only)

## Overview
A crowd-sized, sliceable chuck roast in a glossy red-wine gravy—built entirely in the Instant Pot. Pressure until tender, rest the beef, then cook the vegetables briefly so they stay intact. Finish with a quick reduction for a restaurant-level sauce.

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** 8–12 servings (from a 4½–5½ lb chuck roast)  
- **Total time:** ~2–2½ hours (includes heat-up and release)  
- **Pressure cycle (baseline):** **52 minutes High Pressure** + **20 minutes Natural Release** for a ~2½–3" thick roast (see Timing Notes)

## Equipment
- 6–8 qt Instant Pot (or similar electric pressure cooker)  
- Tongs, wooden spoon, measuring cup/spoons  
- Small bowl for slurry (optional)  
- Cutting board and foil for resting/slicing

## Ingredients (in use-order)
- 1 **chuck roast**, 4½–5½ lb, patted very dry  
- 2½ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste  
- 2 tsp freshly ground black pepper  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 2 large yellow onions, halved and sliced ½"  
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced  
- 2 tbsp tomato paste  
- 1 cup (240 ml) dry red wine, for deglazing  
- 1 cup (240 ml) low-sodium beef stock  
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce (or 1 tsp soy sauce), optional  
- 4 thyme sprigs, 1 rosemary sprig, 2 bay leaves  
- 8 oz (225 g) cremini mushrooms, halved (optional)  
- 4 medium carrots, cut into 1½" chunks  
- 1 lb (450 g) baby potatoes, halved  
- 1–2 tbsp chopped parsley (optional, garnish)

> **Thickening option:** 2 tbsp (16 g) cornstarch + 2 tbsp (30 ml) cold water, whisked (or rely on reduction only).

## Instructions
1. **Season & Sear (Sauté – High, ~8–10 min)**  
   - Season roast all over with the salt and pepper.  
   - Set IP to **Sauté (High)**; add oil. Sear roast 3–4 minutes per side until deeply browned. Transfer to a plate (leave drippings).

2. **Build the Fond (Sauté – Medium, ~6–8 min)**  
   - Add onions; cook, stirring, until softened and lightly golden, 4–5 minutes.  
   - Add garlic; cook 30–45 seconds until fragrant.  
   - Push veg to the side; add **tomato paste** and cook 60 seconds, stirring, until darkened.

3. **Deglaze**  
   - Pour in **red wine**; scrape up every browned bit with a wooden spoon until the bottom is perfectly clean. Simmer 1–2 minutes to reduce slightly.  
   - Stir in **beef stock** and **Worcestershire/soy** (if using). Add **thyme, rosemary, bay leaves**. Return roast (and any juices) to the pot, nestling it so at least the bottom third is in liquid. Add mushrooms now if using.

4. **Pressure Cook the Beef (High 52 min + 20 min Natural Release)**  
   - Lock lid; **Valve: Sealing**. Pressure cook **52 minutes (High)**.  
   - Let **Natural Release 20 minutes**, then quick-release remaining pressure.  
   - Transfer roast to a board; tent with foil and **rest 10–15 minutes**.

5. **Cook the Vegetables (so they don’t mush)**  
   - Skim excess fat if you like; bring the juices to a simmer on **Sauté**.  
   - Add **potatoes** and **carrots** (submerge potatoes; stack carrots on top).  
   - Lid on; **High Pressure 4 minutes**, **Quick Release**. Veg should be just tender.

6. **Finish the Sauce**  
   - Switch to **Sauté (High)** and reduce 5–10 minutes to a glossy gravy.  
   - If you prefer thicker, whisk in cornstarch slurry in thirds, simmering 30–60 seconds after each addition until it lightly coats a spoon. Taste; adjust salt/pepper.

7. **Slice & Serve**  
   - Slice roast **across the grain** into thick slices (or large chunks).  
   - Return slices to the pot to glaze in sauce, or arrange on a platter and ladle sauce/veg over. Garnish with parsley.

## Timing Notes & Doneness
- **Thickness beats weight.** For ~**3" thick** chuck, **52–54 min High** + 20 min natural release yields sliceable-tender. Around **2" thick** often needs ~45 minutes; significantly thicker roasts may need **60–65 minutes**.  
- If a test slice seems tight or dry, it’s usually **under-** not over-cooked connective tissue: return to **High Pressure 8–10 minutes** with a short natural release.

## Troubleshooting
- **“Burn” message during sauté/pressure:** Deglaze thoroughly after tomato paste and before pressure—viscous tomato products can scorch if stuck to the bottom. Add a splash more stock and scrape until the base is clean.  
- **Meat is tough after the cycle:** Close, cook **8–10 minutes** more at High, then natural release 10.  
- **Sauce too thin:** Reduce on **Sauté** 5–10 minutes, or add slurry in small additions.  
- **Too salty:** Balance with ½–1 cup additional unsalted stock and reduce again; avoid salty stocks/packet mixes up front.

## Make-Ahead & Reheating
- **1–2 days ahead:** Chill roast **whole** in its sauce (easier to slice cold). Next day, slice and **reheat gently on Sauté (Low)** or **Keep Warm** in the sauce until hot. Flavor improves day 2.  
- **Same-day hold:** Leave pot on **Keep Warm** with the roast submerged in sauce; cook vegetables in a quick second cycle just before serving.

## Notes
- **Wine choice:** Use a dry, medium-bodied red you like to drink (Cabernet, Merlot, Côtes du Rhône). Reducing after pressure drives off harsh notes and concentrates flavor.  
- **Mushroom add-in:** For a bourguignon vibe, include the mushrooms before pressure or brown separately and fold in at the end.  
- **Umami bump (optional):** A teaspoon of soy sauce deepens savoriness without reading “soy.”
