# Apple Cider Doughnut Loaf (9x5) - v4.1 (Spice-Forward, Cider-Soaked, Cinnamon-Sugar Coated)

## Title & Overview
A tender, spice-forward loaf designed to taste like an apple cider doughnut: cider is reduced for concentration, baked into the batter, then reinforced with a warm reduced-cider soak and a cider-butter brush that locks on cinnamon sugar. The “reduced cider in batter + soak” mechanism is the defining feature of the reference method.

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: 1 loaf (9x5), about 8-10 slices
- Active Prep: about 30 minutes
- Hands-Off/Rest: about 40 minutes (cool + soak + cool before slicing)
- Cook: about 60-80 minutes (bake)
- Total: about 1 hr 50 min to 2 hr 30 min (mostly driven by reduction + bake)

## Ingredients (in use-order)

### A) Apple cider reduction (measured endpoint)
- Apple cider, 2 1/2 cups, reduced to exactly 3/4 cup

After reducing to 3/4 cup, split:
- For batter: 1/2 cup reduced cider
- Reserved for finishing: 1/4 cup reduced cider total, divided:
  - 3 Tbsp for soak
  - 1 Tbsp for cider-butter brush

### B) Dry mix
- All-purpose flour, 1 3/4 cups (about 210 g)
- Cornstarch, 2 Tbsp (about 15 g)
- Baking powder, 1 1/4 tsp
- Baking soda, 1/2 tsp
- Kosher salt, 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal (or 1/4 tsp Morton)

Spice-forward batter blend (nutmeg reduced)
- Ground cinnamon, 1 Tbsp
- Ground nutmeg, 1/2 tsp
- Apple pie spice, 1/2 tsp

### C) Wet mix
- Neutral oil, 1/3 cup
- Light brown sugar (packed), 2/3 cup
- Granulated sugar, 1/4 cup
- Large eggs, 2
- Large egg yolk, 1
- Sour cream, 1/2 cup
- Vanilla extract, 1 tsp
- Reduced cider (from above), 1/2 cup

### D) Finish
Soak
- Reserved reduced cider, 3 Tbsp

Cider-butter brush
- Unsalted butter, 2 Tbsp, melted
- Reserved reduced cider, 1 Tbsp

Cinnamon-sugar coat (enough for top + sides)
- Granulated sugar, 1/2 cup
- Ground cinnamon, 1 Tbsp
- Ground nutmeg, 1/4 tsp
- Big pinch kosher salt

## Detailed Instructions (with time estimates)

1) Reduce cider to an exact volume (20-35 minutes total)
   - (2 min) Add 2 1/2 cups cider to a saucepan; bring to a boil.
   - (15-25 min) Reduce to a lively simmer, uncovered, until you have exactly 3/4 cup.
     - Measure to confirm; do not eyeball.
   - (1 min) Split immediately:
     - 1/2 cup for batter
     - 1/4 cup reserved for finishing
   - (5 min, passive) Let the 1/2 cup (batter portion) cool about 5 minutes (warm is good; hot is not).

2) Prep oven + pan (5 minutes)
   - (2 min) Heat oven to 325°F, rack middle.
   - (3 min) Grease a 9x5 pan and line with a parchment sling; set pan on a rimmed baking sheet.

3) Mix cinnamon-sugar coat (2 minutes)
   - (2 min) Whisk together:
     - 1/2 cup sugar
     - 1 Tbsp cinnamon
     - 1/4 tsp nutmeg
     - big pinch salt
   - Set aside.

4) Mix dry ingredients (3 minutes)
   - (3 min) In a medium bowl, whisk until uniform:
     - flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda, salt
     - 1 Tbsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp nutmeg, 1/2 tsp apple pie spice

5) Mix wet ingredients (7-9 minutes)
   - (2 min) In a small bowl, whisk until smooth:
     - sour cream + vanilla + 1/2 cup warm (not hot) reduced cider
   - (2 min) In a large bowl, whisk oil + brown sugar + 1/4 cup granulated sugar until glossy.
   - (2-3 min) Whisk in eggs + yolk until cohesive and slightly thickened.
   - (1-2 min) Whisk in the sour cream-cider mixture.

6) Combine batter (2-3 minutes)
   - (2-3 min) Add dry mix to wet mix; fold/whisk just until no dry pockets remain.
     - Stop mixing as soon as homogeneous.

7) Bake (60-80 minutes; check early)
   - (1 min) Pour batter into pan; level top.
   - (60-80 min) Bake at 325°F.
     - Start checking at 55-60 minutes.
     - Doneness cues:
       - toothpick in the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs (not wet batter)
       - center springs back when pressed lightly
       - optional: internal temp 200-205°F in the center
     - If top browns early: tent loosely with foil for the last 10-20 minutes.

8) Soak in-pan, then lift out (about 20 minutes total; mostly passive)
   - (10 min, passive) Cool loaf in the pan on a rack for 10 minutes.
   - (2 min) While still in the pan:
     - poke the top all over (30-40 holes)
     - spoon 3 Tbsp reserved reduced cider evenly over the top
   - (10 min, passive) Rest 10 minutes to absorb.
   - (1 min) Lift loaf out by parchment onto a rack over a rimmed sheet pan.

9) Brush + coat (5-7 minutes)
   - (1 min) Stir 1 Tbsp reserved reduced cider into 2 Tbsp melted butter.
   - (2 min) Brush top and sides while loaf is still warm.
   - (2-4 min) Coat with cinnamon sugar on all sides; press lightly to adhere.
   - (20 min, passive) Cool at least 20 minutes before slicing.

## Troubleshooting
- Gummy center: underbaked (common in loaf geometry). Keep baking; tent foil if top darkens; target 200-205°F center.
- Tight/dense crumb: overmixed batter. Mix only until just combined.
- Soak runs off: too few holes / loaf too cool. Poke more; soak while still in pan and warm; spoon slowly.
- Coating won’t stick: brushed too late / loaf cooled. Brush and coat immediately after soak rest.
