# Peanut Butter + Apple Butter Oat-Fiber Spread (Fridge-Stable, 1+ Week)

## Title & Overview
A sweet-leaning, higher-fiber peanut-butter spread designed for sourdough toast. Toasted-and-ground rolled oats (plus a little ground flax) add body and fiber without turning it into a bar or paste. Apple butter brings sweetness and a mild tang that reads "breakfasty" instead of candy.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup old-fashioned rolled oats (dry)
- 1 cup natural creamy peanut butter
- 1/2 cup apple butter
- 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- 1/8 tsp kosher salt (optional; consider skipping if sodium-sensitive)
- 1 tsp apple cider vinegar (optional; brightens flavor slightly)

## Allergy & Dietary Notes
- Contains: peanuts.
- Spread itself is typically gluten-free, but confirm oat handling if you care about certified GF.
- Apple butter sweetness varies widely by brand (and some contain added sugar).

## Quick Overview
1) Toast rolled oats until fragrant, then cool and grind into a coarse flour.
2) Stir ground oats + flax + cinnamon into peanut butter until smooth.
3) Fold in apple butter (and optional vanilla/vinegar). Rest 5 minutes, adjust texture.
4) Jar and refrigerate. Use within 7 to 10 days for best flavor.

## Instructions
1) Toast the oats
- Heat a dry skillet over medium heat.
- Add the rolled oats and stir frequently for 4 to 7 minutes, until they smell nutty and lightly toasted.
- Immediately transfer to a plate to cool for 5 minutes (prevents over-toasting).

2) Grind the oats
- Grind cooled toasted oats in a blender/spice grinder/food processor.
- Target: mostly flour with some fine grit (this keeps it spreadable and not gummy).

3) Build the base
- In a mixing bowl, add:
  - peanut butter
  - ground toasted oats
  - ground flaxseed
  - cinnamon
  - (optional) vanilla
- Stir until fully combined. It will thicken as the oat/flax hydrates.

4) Add sweetness and balance
- Fold in the apple butter.
- (Optional) Add apple cider vinegar for a slightly brighter finish.
- Let sit 5 minutes, then re-stir.

5) Texture adjustment (choose one)
- Too thick: add 1 to 2 tsp more apple butter, or 1 to 2 tsp warm water, then re-stir and rest 2 minutes.
- Too loose: add 1 tbsp more ground toasted oats, rest 5 minutes, then re-stir.

6) Store
- Scrape into a clean jar, seal, refrigerate.
- Stir before each use (especially if using very natural peanut butter).

## Common Issues
- "It tastes flat": add vanilla (1/2 tsp) and/or vinegar (1 tsp). Cinnamon also scales well up to 1 1/2 tsp.
- "It is gritty": grind oats finer, or let the jar rest overnight; oats soften over time.
- "It is too stiff out of the fridge": let the jar sit 5 to 10 minutes at room temp, then stir.

## Make-Ahead Notes
- Fridge life: 7 to 10 days (often longer, but flavor is best in that window).
- Use a clean spoon every time. Keep the lid sealed to avoid fridge odors.

## Reheat Plan
- No reheat needed.
- If firm: 5 to 10 minutes at room temp, then stir.

## Troubleshooting
- If the spread separates: this is normal with natural peanut butter. Stir vigorously; if it keeps separating, increase ground oats by 1 tbsp next time.
- If it becomes too thick after 24 hours: thin with 1 to 2 tsp apple butter or water, then rest 2 minutes.

## Troubleshooting Table
| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Too thick | Oats/flax absorbed more moisture | Add 1 to 2 tsp apple butter or water |
| Too thin | Not enough ground oats or too much apple butter | Add 1 tbsp ground oats, rest 5 min |
| Gritty | Oats ground too coarse | Grind finer, or rest overnight |
| Bland | Peanut butter brand is mild | Add vanilla and/or vinegar; increase cinnamon |

## Geometry Notes
- Typical toast: 1 to 2 tbsp per slice of sourdough.
- If you want it "PB-thick" on toast, plan on 3 tbsp per slice (nutrition scales linearly).

## Scaling & Batch Size
- 2x batch fits well in a standard blender/food processor for grinding oats.
- If scaling up, toast oats in batches so they toast evenly.

## Special Notes
- This is intentionally low-sodium unless you add salt. If you crave "PB cookie" vibes, vanilla helps more than extra sugar.
- If you want a stronger apple-cinnamon note, increase cinnamon rather than increasing apple butter (which can make it looser).

## Variations
1) Higher fiber (still spreadable)
- Increase ground flaxseed to 3 tbsp.
- Increase ground toasted oats to 2/3 cup (toast/grind 2/3 cup oats).
- Add 1 to 2 tbsp extra apple butter to keep it spreadable.

2) Sweeter
- Increase apple butter to 2/3 cup.
- Add 1/2 tsp vanilla (if not already).
- Expect a looser spread; add 1 tbsp extra ground oats if needed.

3) "PBJ" direction (still fridge-stable)
- Swap half the apple butter for a thick jam/preserves.
- Add 1 tsp vinegar to keep it from tasting cloying.

## Safety & Correctness Notes
- Peanut allergy risk is serious: avoid cross-contact if serving others.
- Ground flax can go rancid over time; keep sealed and refrigerated, and discard if it smells painty/bitter.
- Always use a clean utensil to avoid introducing moisture/crumbs that shorten shelf life.
