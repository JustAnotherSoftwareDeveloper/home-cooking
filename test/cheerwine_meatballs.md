# Cheerwine-Glazed Instant Pot Meatballs (Football Sunday)

## Overview
Juicy party-size meatballs, pressure-cooked on a trivet (no Burn issues) and lacquered in a glossy **Cheerwine + apple-cider-vinegar** BBQ glaze. Built to hold on “Keep Warm,” great with toothpicks or over rice.

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** ~48–54 appetizer meatballs (1–1¼″ each)  
- **Active time:** ~25 minutes  
- **Total time:** 45–55 minutes (includes pressure + NR and glaze reduction)

## Ingredients (use-order)

### Meatballs
- ½ cup panko or breadcrumbs (GF if needed)  
- ½ cup milk (or water)  
- 2 lb ground beef  
- ½ lb ground pork (optional)  
- 2 large eggs  
- 2 tsp kosher salt  
- 1 tsp black pepper  
- ½ tsp smoked paprika  
- 1 tsp onion powder (omit for low-FODMAP)  
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley (plus more to finish)  
- **IP liquid:** 1 cup beef broth or water (6-qt) or 1½ cups (8-qt)

### Cheerwine BBQ Glaze (game-day scale)
- 1½ cups Cheerwine  
- ¾ cup ketchup  
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar  
- 1 small garlic clove, finely minced (or 1 tsp garlic-infused oil)  
- ½ tsp each: chili powder, ground cumin, dried oregano, paprika  
- Pinch ground allspice  
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce  
- 1–2 tsp hot sauce, to taste  
- Lemon juice, to taste (optional)

## Make-Ahead Plan

### Day-Before (Do-Ahead, ~30–40 min)
1. **Make the glaze:** Simmer **1½ cups Cheerwine** to reduce by ~⅓ (to ~1 cup). Whisk in ketchup, vinegar, garlic (or infused oil), chili powder, cumin, oregano, paprika, allspice, Worcestershire, and hot sauce. Simmer 3–5 min until **glossy and coats a spoon**; balance with lemon/salt. Cool, jar, refrigerate.  
2. **Mix & shape meatballs:** Hydrate breadcrumbs with milk (2 min). Add beef (+ pork if using), eggs, salt, pepper, smoked paprika, onion powder (if using), parsley. Mix gently; shape **1–1¼″** balls. Arrange on a parchment-lined sheet; cover tightly and refrigerate **up to 24 hours**.  
3. **Prep gear:** Lightly oil the IP trivet; measure thin liquid (1 cup for 6-qt, 1½ cups for 8-qt). Foil-line a sheet pan and set oven rack to upper-middle if doing a quick broil finish.  
4. **Safety:** Keep raw meatballs ≤40°F; store glaze covered and chilled. Don’t store meatballs in sauce.

### Cook-Day (25–35 min active; 45–55 min total)
1. **Load the pot:** Add thin liquid to the Instant Pot. Insert trivet; arrange meatballs (don’t pack tight).  
2. **Pressure cook:** **High Pressure 9 minutes**, then **Natural Release 5 minutes**. Check a center meatball—aim **160°F**; if needed, reseal and cook **+1–2 minutes**.  
3. **Warm the glaze (parallel):** Reheat the do-ahead glaze until fluid and shiny.  
4. **Glaze & finish:** Toss hot meatballs with **½–⅔** of the glaze.  
   - **One-pot lacquer:** Return to IP on **Sauté**, toss **1–2 min** until shiny.  
   - **Crispy edges (optional):** Broil on a foil-lined sheet **1–2 min**, then brush with more glaze.  
5. **Hold & serve:** Keep on **Keep Warm** (≥140°F). If thickening during hold, add a splash of hot water or more glaze; garnish with parsley/scallion greens. Serve extra glaze on the side.

## Troubleshooting
- **Burn notice (thick sauces):** Pressure-cook meatballs **over** thin liquid on a trivet; glaze after.  
- **Glaze too thin:** Simmer longer, or whisk **1 tsp cornstarch + 1 tsp water** into simmering glaze.  
- **Too sweet:** Add 1–2 tsp apple cider vinegar + a few dashes hot sauce; pinch of salt.  
- **Dense/tough meatballs:** Mix less; ensure panade is hydrated; add 1–2 tbsp water to mixture if needed.  
- **Sticking to trivet:** Lightly oil trivet; allow **5-minute NR** before opening so proteins relax.  
- **Holding dries them out:** Toss with a bit more glaze or hot water; keep covered at **≥140°F**.

## Notes & Variations
- **Low-FODMAP toggles:** Use garlic-infused oil in glaze; skip onion powder; choose lactose-free milk (or water) in meatballs; use a ketchup that works for you.  
- **Broiler char:** 1–2 minutes gives restaurant-style browned spots without drying.  
- **Serving ideas:** Over buttered rice; with toothpicks; or in slider rolls.

## Storage & Safety
- Refrigerate leftovers within **2 hours**; keep hot foods **≥140°F** on the buffet.  
- Store 3–4 days in the fridge; reheat to steaming hot before serving.
