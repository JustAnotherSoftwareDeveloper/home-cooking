# Thai Red Curry Tofu and Veggie Bowl Pucks (1 cup each)

## Title & Overview
These are 1-cup freezer pucks of Thai-inspired red curry tofu and vegetables designed to be the “bowl” part of a rice bowl: microwave a puck, add hot rice, and eat. The tofu stays tofu (firm cubes) and the curry is thickened to be spoon-coating so it unmolds cleanly and reheats without turning soupy. This version is intentionally lighter than restaurant curry: it uses a modest amount of coconut milk plus broth for flavor, then relies on reduction and a small cornstarch slurry for structure.

## Allergy & Dietary Notes
- **Contains:** soy, coconut
- **Easy swaps (keep technique intact):**
  - Soy sauce -> tamari -> no technique change.
  - Broth -> water -> slightly cleaner flavor; you may want a touch more lime at the end.
- **Hard swaps (not recommended / major change):**
  - Skipping thickening (no reduction, no slurry) -> pucks will be fragile and watery after reheat.
- **Diet toggles (optional; only if relevant to the dish):**
  - Low-sodium: use water, start at 4 tbsp curry paste, keep soy sauce to 1 tbsp, push lime and herbs at the end.

## Quick Overview
- **What you are making:** Thick Thai-inspired red curry tofu and vegetables portioned into 1-cup freezer pucks to serve over hot rice.
- **Texture and doneness cues:** Sauce should be spoon-coating; tofu cubes should hold shape; green beans should be just tender-crisp before freezing.
- **Workflow:** Brown tofu in the pot, build curry base in the same fat, simmer veg in stages, thicken, cool, portion, freeze. *While the curry simmers, set up molds on a sheet pan and mix the slurry.*
- **Make-ahead:** Freeze as 1-cup pucks once sauce is thick and veg is slightly underdone so it finishes nicely on reheat.
- **Reheat plan:** Microwave from frozen, stir once mid-way, and reheat until steaming hot throughout; then spoon over rice.
- **Primary risks:** Sauce too thin (pucks crack), curry too salty (paste varies), veg too soft (overcooked before freezing).

## Instructions
1. **Brown the tofu for better freeze texture**
   - Press tofu 15 minutes, then cut into `3/4-in` cubes and pat dry.
   - In a Dutch oven over **medium-high heat**, heat avocado oil until shimmering.
   - Add tofu in a single layer (work in 2 batches if needed).
   - Brown 8 to 12 minutes total, turning occasionally, until edges are lightly golden and cubes feel firmer.
   - Remove tofu to a bowl; leave the thin oil film in the pot.
   - **Checkpoint:** tofu has visible golden edges and no longer looks wet on the surface.

2. **Build the curry base (bloom the paste)**
   - Add onion; saute over **medium heat** 3 to 5 minutes until softened.
   - Add garlic + ginger; saute 30 to 60 seconds until fragrant.
   - Add curry paste; fry 60 to 90 seconds, stirring constantly, until aromatic and glossy.
   - If paste threatens to scorch, splash in 1 to 2 tbsp broth and scrape fond.

3. **Simmer the curry and cook vegetables in stages**
   - Add coconut milk + broth; whisk until smooth; bring to a gentle simmer.
   - Add tofu back in; simmer 2 to 3 minutes.
   - Add carrots + green beans; simmer 5 to 7 minutes (aim slightly underdone).
   - Add bell peppers + bamboo shoots; simmer 3 to 4 minutes.
   - **Checkpoint:** carrots nearly tender; beans tender-crisp; peppers softened but not collapsing.

4. **Thicken, finish, and taste-adjust (puck-structure step)**
   - If the curry looks thin, reduce uncovered 2 to 5 minutes first.
   - Whisk cornstarch + cold water into a slurry; stir into simmering curry.
   - Simmer 60 to 90 seconds until glossy and spoon-coating.
   - Stir in spinach until wilted (30 to 60 seconds).
   - Season: soy sauce + optional brown sugar + lime juice. Taste and adjust.
   - **Checkpoint:** sauce clings to tofu/veg; spoon trail closes slowly.

5. **Portion into 1-cup molds and freeze**
   - Cool 10 to 15 minutes.
   - Place molds on a sheet pan.
   - Portion 1 cup into each mold, distributing tofu/veg evenly.
   - Freeze until solid, then pop out and store pucks in a freezer bag or airtight container.

## Make-Ahead Notes
- **Fridge:** 3 to 4 days airtight; reheat until steaming hot; finish with lime/herbs.
- **Freezer:** best quality 2 to 3 months. Freeze as pucks once fully thickened.

## Reheat Plan (from frozen)
- Place 1 puck in a microwave-safe bowl and cover.
- Microwave on High **3:30 to 5:30 minutes**, stirring once mid-way if possible.
- Heat until **steaming hot throughout**, then spoon over hot rice.
- If sauce looks split, stir hard while hot; add 1 tbsp hot water if needed.

## Troubleshooting
- **Too watery:** simmer uncovered 2 to 6 minutes (before molding), or add a small extra slurry.
- **Too salty:** dilute with water/broth, re-simmer 1 minute, then re-balance with lime.
- **Veg mushy after reheat:** undercook beans/peppers slightly next batch.
