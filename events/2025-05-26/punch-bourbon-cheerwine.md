# Cheerwine Bourbon Cherry BBQ Pitcher Punch

## Title & Overview
This is a BBQ-ready pitcher punch built around **bourbon, Cheerwine, cherry liqueur, lemon, and bitters**. It drinks like a Southern cherry-cola whiskey highball: fizzy, cold, bourbon-present, cherry-forward, and not tropical. The formula is a **BBQ pitcher adaptation**, not a direct scale, of Cheerwine’s Porch Sipper, which uses Cheerwine, bourbon, lemon juice, and cherry bitters [1]. The cherry liqueur direction is supported by Cheerwine’s bourbon smash, which uses Luxardo, bourbon, bitters, cherries, lemon, mint, and a Cheerwine top-off [2]. Carbonation is added only at service because batched fizzy cocktails lose quality if the soda goes in too early [5][6].

## Yield & Timing
- **Yield:** 1 pitcher; about **56 oz punch**, enough for **8 drinks at about 7 oz each before glass ice**
- **Alcohol strength:** each 7 oz serving contains about **1 1/2 oz bourbon + 1/4 oz cherry liqueur before ice dilution**
- **Active Prep:** 10 to 15 minutes
- **Inactive Prep / Hands-Off / Rest:** 30 minutes to 4 hours chilling
- **Cook:** none
- **Total:** 40 minutes minimum; best with 2 to 4 hours chilling for the still base
- **Make-ahead:** Bourbon, cherry liqueur, and bitters can be batched up to 3 days ahead. Add lemon juice day-of, ideally within a few hours of serving. Add cold Cheerwine only at service.

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups bourbon
- 1/4 cup Cherry Heering **or** 3 tablespoons Luxardo Maraschino
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice, from about 4 lemons
- 8 dashes Angostura bitters
- 16 dashes cherry bitters
- 4 3/4 cups cold Cheerwine, added at service
- 12 to 16 cocktail cherries, for garnish
- Lemon wedges or wheels, for garnish
- Ice, for serving in glasses

## Allergy & Dietary Notes
- **Contains:** alcohol.
- **Easy swaps:**
  - Cherry Heering -> Luxardo Maraschino -> drier, nuttier, less jammy cherry profile -> use 3 tablespoons instead of 1/4 cup.
  - Bourbon -> rye -> sharper and less sweet -> keep the same volume.
  - Regular Cheerwine -> Cheerwine Zero Sugar -> less sweet, thinner body -> add 1 to 2 teaspoons cocktail cherry syrup only if the finished punch tastes too lean.
- **Hard swaps:**
  - Cheerwine -> generic cherry soda -> loses the specific cherry-cola profile -> only use if Cheerwine is unavailable, then increase bitters by 2 to 4 dashes.
  - Pineapple juice or ginger ale -> changes the drink into holiday/church punch -> not recommended for this recipe.
- **Cross-contact note:** bitters and liqueurs vary by brand; check labels if serving guests with strict allergen or gluten concerns.

## Quick Overview
- **What you are making:** A cold, fizzy bourbon-Cheerwine pitcher punch with cherry liqueur, lemon, and bitters.
- **Texture and doneness cues:** The finished punch should be visibly bubbly, cold, lightly tart, cherry-forward, and bourbon-present without tasting sticky.
- **Workflow:** Batch the still ingredients first; while the base chills, cut garnishes and keep the Cheerwine cold.
- **Make-ahead:** The spirit-and-bitters base can be made ahead, but lemon is best day-of and Cheerwine goes in at service.
- **Primary risks:** Too sweet, too flat, too boozy before ice dilution, or too medicinal from excess cherry liqueur.

## Gather & Stage
- Chill the Cheerwine completely.
- Chill the bourbon and cherry liqueur if convenient.
- Juice the lemons and strain out seeds.
- Cut lemon wedges or wheels.
- Set out the pitcher, measuring tools, glasses, ice, cocktail cherries, and garnishes.
- **Checkpoint:** Everything is cold, measured, and ready before the Cheerwine is opened.

## Instructions

1. Build the still base
   - **Combine** the bourbon, cherry liqueur, fresh lemon juice, Angostura bitters, and cherry bitters in the pitcher.
     - Stir gently for 10 to 15 seconds, just until uniform.
     - Do not add Cheerwine yet.
     - **Checkpoint:** The base should smell like bourbon, cherry, lemon, and bitters, not straight cherry candy.
     - *If the base smells aggressively sweet -> add 1 tablespoon lemon juice -> cue it is fixed when the lemon is noticeable but not sour.*

2. Chill the base
   - **Chill** the pitcher base for at least 30 minutes.
     - Best BBQ service window: chill the still base 2 to 4 hours.
     - For overnight prep, batch bourbon, cherry liqueur, and bitters only; add lemon juice day-of.
     - **Checkpoint:** The base should be refrigerator-cold before the Cheerwine goes in.
     - *If the base tastes harsh when cold -> do not sweeten yet; plan to serve over packed ice -> cue it is fixed when the first test pour softens after 30 to 60 seconds over ice.*

3. Add Cheerwine at service
   - **Pour** the cold Cheerwine slowly into the pitcher.
     - Pour down the inside wall of the pitcher to reduce foaming.
     - Stir gently 2 to 3 turns; do not whip, shake, or churn.
     - **Checkpoint:** The pitcher should still show visible carbonation after mixing.
     - *If the punch foams hard -> stop pouring for 30 seconds, then continue slowly -> cue it is fixed when the foam drops and the liquid level stabilizes.*

4. Serve over ice
   - **Fill** glasses with ice, then pour the punch over the ice.
     - Garnish each glass with a cocktail cherry and lemon wedge or wheel.
     - Keep the pitcher cold between pours.
     - Do not add ice directly to the pitcher unless the entire pitcher will be served within 15 minutes.
     - **Checkpoint:** Each glass should be cold and lightly diluted by the time the guest drinks it.
     - *If the pitcher starts tasting flat during service -> top each glass with a small splash of fresh cold Cheerwine -> cue it is fixed when bubbles return at the rim.*

5. Finish and taste-adjust
   - **Taste** one glass after it sits over ice for 30 to 60 seconds.
     - Taste-adjust loop: acid -> sweetness/carbonation -> bitterness -> spirit strength.
     - For more acid: add lemon juice 1 tablespoon at a time to the pitcher.
     - For more sweetness/carbonation: add cold Cheerwine 1/4 cup at a time.
     - For more structure: add Angostura or cherry bitters 2 dashes at a time.
     - For more strength: add bourbon 2 tablespoons at a time.
     - **Checkpoint:** Final punch is cherry-cola forward, bourbon-present, lightly tart, fizzy, and not sticky.

## Common Issues
- **Too sweet:** Cheerwine plus cherry liqueur is concentrated -> add lemon juice 1 tablespoon at a time -> finish tastes cleaner.
- **Too flat:** Cheerwine was added too early or stirred too hard -> top each glass with fresh cold Cheerwine -> bubbles return at the rim.
- **Too boozy:** The punch was tasted before proper ice dilution -> serve over packed ice and wait 30 to 60 seconds -> bourbon integrates.
- **Too medicinal:** Cherry liqueur is too dominant -> add lemon and Cheerwine in small increments -> cherry shifts back into the background.

## Make-Ahead Notes
- Fridge plan:
  - Stop after Step 1 if serving the same day.
  - For overnight prep, batch only bourbon, cherry liqueur, and bitters.
  - Holds: still base without lemon holds up to 3 days refrigerated.
  - Add day-of: lemon juice within a few hours of service, then Cheerwine immediately before serving.
- BBQ service plan:
  - Keep Cheerwine bottles unopened and cold until the pitcher goes out.
  - Keep the pitcher in the refrigerator or cooler between refills.
  - Refill trigger: if the pitcher drops below half-full, gently stir once before pouring more.
- Freezer plan:
  - Do not freeze the full punch.
  - Optional only: freeze a little extra Cheerwine into cubes if you specifically want less watery glass dilution; expect reduced carbonation and a softer texture after melting.

## Troubleshooting
- Punch tastes syrupy -> cherry liqueur and Cheerwine are outweighing acid -> add lemon juice 1 tablespoon at a time -> finish becomes cleaner and less sticky.
- Punch tastes harsh -> not enough chill or ice dilution -> serve over packed ice and wait 30 to 60 seconds -> bourbon integrates.
- Punch goes flat -> carbonated Cheerwine was added too early or stirred too hard -> top glasses with fresh cold Cheerwine -> visible bubbles return.
- Punch tastes thin -> too much ice melt or too much Cheerwine -> add 2 tablespoons bourbon and 1 teaspoon cherry liqueur per 2 cups punch -> cherry-bourbon body returns.

## Geometry Notes
- Pitcher size used: **2-quart minimum**
- Target batch volume: about **56 oz before glass ice**
- Headspace rule: a 2-quart / 64 oz pitcher leaves about **8 oz headspace**, enough for gentle stirring and carbonation foam.
- Crowding rule: do not add ice to the pitcher for normal service; ice belongs in glasses.
- Service depth: keep the pitcher below roughly 90% full after adding Cheerwine.

## Scaling & Batch Size
- Half batch:
  - 3/4 cup bourbon
  - 2 tablespoons Cherry Heering or 1 1/2 tablespoons Luxardo Maraschino
  - 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  - 4 dashes Angostura bitters
  - 8 dashes cherry bitters
  - 2 3/8 cups cold Cheerwine
- Double batch:
  - Use two 2-quart pitchers instead of one large dispenser if possible.
  - Add Cheerwine to each pitcher only when that pitcher goes out.
  - Do not fully carbonate a large dispenser hours ahead; it will flatten.
- Stronger batch:
  - Increase bourbon to 1 3/4 cups.
  - Do not increase cherry liqueur unless you also increase lemon; cherry liqueur is the fastest path to syrupy punch.

## Special Notes
- Cherry Heering is the better default if you want a deeper, jammy cherry profile; Luxardo Maraschino is drier, clearer, and more almond/nut-like, so it should be used in a slightly smaller amount [8][9].
- The drink is deliberately lighter than a direct Porch Sipper scale. A direct 8-drink scale of the official single drink would use 2 cups bourbon and 4 cups Cheerwine; this BBQ version uses 1 1/2 cups bourbon, adds cherry liqueur, and keeps the pour more casual for self-service [1].
- Fresh herbs are not built into the pitcher. If you want mint, garnish individual glasses only; herbs can get bitter or tired if they sit in a batch too long [7].

## Variations
1. **Cleaner Porch Sipper Pitcher:** Drop the cherry liqueur and increase bourbon to 2 cups. Keep the lemon and Cheerwine the same, and increase cherry bitters to 24 to 32 dashes for a cleaner bourbon-Cheerwine profile.

2. **Drier Cocktail-Bar Version:** Use rye instead of bourbon and Luxardo Maraschino instead of Cherry Heering. The punch becomes sharper, less jammy, and less obviously sweet.

3. **Deeper Cherry BBQ Punch:** Use Cherry Heering and add 1 tablespoon syrup from the cocktail cherry jar. This is richer and more cherry-forward; add an extra 1 to 2 tablespoons lemon if it starts to taste sticky.

4. **Lower-ABV BBQ Pitcher:** Reduce bourbon to 1 cup and cherry liqueur to 2 tablespoons. Increase Cheerwine to 5 1/4 cups so it still fills the pitcher properly.

## Safety & Correctness Notes
- Label the pitcher clearly as alcoholic.
- Keep a non-alcoholic Cheerwine bottle separate so guests can dilute or top off their own glass.
- Keep the pitcher cold during BBQ service and refill in smaller waves rather than leaving a full pitcher in the heat.
- Do not add Cheerwine until service; multiple batching guides specifically warn that carbonated ingredients should be added at the end to preserve fizz [5][6][7].
