# Session B Integrated Runbook — Kofta Wraps, Picadillo Burritos, and Picadillo Pupusas

## Scope
This is one start-to-finish production document for the three handheld freezer meals:
- **5 Turkish-inspired kofta wraps**
- **5 freezer picadillo burritos**
- **10 picadillo beef pupusas** (5 servings; 2 pupusas per serving)

This document combines the runbook and the recipes into one workflow so you can execute the whole session without jumping between files.

## Output
By the end of this session, you should have:
- **5 wrapped kofta wraps**
- **5 wrapped burritos**
- **10 cooked pupusas**, packed as 5 servings
- **optional:** 1 separate container of kofta yogurt sauce in the fridge, only if you expect to eat some wraps within the next 48 hours

## Realistic time estimate
### Practical estimate
- **About 4 hr 15 min to 5 hr**

### Best-case, low-friction execution
- **About 4 hr 15 min to 4 hr 45 min** if setup is clean, the freezer has room for the burrito tray, and you do not lose time to cleanup drag, tray reshuffling, or shaping delays.

## Core strategy
These three recipes all depend on the same structural logic:
1. Cook the filling or protein dry enough to hold.
2. Cool it before shaping or wrapping.
3. Shape or wrap.
4. Cool again when needed.
5. Pack for freezer storage.

The main optimization is to use the passive windows deliberately:
- **Kofta vegetable-base cooling** launches the burrito filling.
- **Burrito filling cooling** launches kofta mixing and chill.
- **Burrito pre-freeze** is used for the pupusa filling.
- **Pupusa filling cooling** is used for the pupusa masa mix/rest.
- **Pupusa masa rest** is used for burrito bagging and kofta wrap assembly.

# Integrated runbook

## Phase 1 — Build the kofta vegetable base
**Estimated time:** 10 to 12 min cook + 15 to 25 min cooling

### What you are doing
You are building the low-moisture vegetable base for the kofta and creating the first passive window.

### Actions
1. In the **Stainless steel pots and pans**, add:
   - 1 1/2 tsp avocado oil
   - minced red bell pepper
   - grated, squeezed-dry zucchini
2. **Saute** over **medium heat** for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring regularly, until most free water is gone and the pan looks closer to glossy than wet.
3. Add:
   - 1 tbsp red pepper paste
   - 1 tbsp tomato paste
4. **Cook** 1 to 2 minutes more, until the pastes darken slightly and smell sweeter, not raw.
5. Spread the mixture onto a plate or shallow tray.
6. Let it cool 15 to 25 minutes, until warm but not hot.

### Parallel task while this cools
Move immediately into Phase 2.

### Checkpoint
The kofta vegetable base should be concentrated, not watery, and no longer steaming.

### If needed
- *If the vegetables still look wet, cook 2 to 4 minutes more; it is fixed when a spatula dragged through the pan leaves a clear path briefly.*

---

## Phase 2 — Cook and reduce the burrito filling
**Estimated time:** 22 to 30 min total + 20 to 25 min cooling

### What you are doing
You are making the tight burrito picadillo and starting the longest mandatory cooling block.

### Actions
1. Reuse the **Stainless steel pots and pans** skillet.
2. Add:
   - 1 Tbsp avocado oil
   - 1 lb ground beef
3. **Brown** over **medium-high heat** for 4 to 6 minutes, breaking the meat into fine crumbles.
4. Spoon off excess fat if the pan looks greasy.
5. Add:
   - diced burrito potato
   - 1 Tbsp tomato paste
   - 1 tsp ground cumin
   - 1 tsp mild chili powder
   - 1/2 tsp dried oregano
   - 1/2 tsp coarse kosher salt
   - 1/4 tsp black pepper
6. **Saute** 1 to 2 minutes over **medium heat**, until the tomato paste darkens slightly and the spices smell toasted.
7. Add:
   - 1/2 cup tomato sauce
   - 1/4 cup water
8. Stir well, bring to a brisk simmer, then reduce to **medium-low heat**.
9. **Simmer partially covered** 10 to 12 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the potatoes are tender.
10. **Uncover** and **reduce** 5 to 8 minutes more, stirring frequently, until the filling is tighter than you would want for a fresh burrito.
11. Spread the finished filling across the **Sheet / baking tray** in a thin layer.
12. Cool 20 to 25 minutes, until no longer steaming.

### Parallel task while the burrito filling cools
As soon as the kofta vegetable base is only warm, start Phase 3.

### Checkpoint
The filling should look compact and lightly glossy, not wet. A spoon dragged through it should leave a visible trail briefly before closing.

### If needed
- *If the potatoes are still firm but the pan is drying out, add 1 Tbsp water, cover 2 minutes more, and test again; it is fixed when the potato yields easily.*
- *If the filling still looks loose, keep reducing uncovered; it is fixed when no free liquid gathers at the edge.*

---

## Phase 3 — Mix, chill, shape, and bake the kofta
**Estimated time:** 5 to 8 min mixing + 15 to 20 min chill + 12 to 16 min bake + 15 to 25 min cooling

### What you are doing
You are using the burrito cooling window to push the kofta all the way through its oven stage.

### Actions
1. In a bowl, combine:
   - 1 lb ground beef
   - 1/2 lb ground lamb
   - cooled kofta vegetable base
   - 1 tsp sweet paprika
   - 3/4 tsp cumin
   - 1/2 tsp coriander
   - 3/4 tsp black pepper
   - 1/2 tsp baking soda
   - 1 tsp kosher salt
   - 1 1/2 tsp chopped parsley
2. Mix by hand 2 to 3 minutes, until tacky and cohesive.
3. **Chill** 15 to 20 minutes so the fat firms and shaping gets easier.
4. Shape into 10 narrow logs, about 4 1/2 in long and roughly 1/2 to 3/4 in thick.
5. Arrange on the **Sheet / baking tray | 13x9 | Metal | Light** with at least 1 inch between pieces.
6. **Bake** at `425 deg F` for 12 to 16 minutes, rotating once halfway, until browned in spots and cooked through.
7. **Broil** 1 to 2 minutes only if the tops still look pale.
8. Transfer the kofta to **Wire racks** and cool 15 to 25 minutes so steam can escape before wrapping.

### Primary use of the kofta chill window
Get ready to roll the burritos as soon as the burrito filling is fully cooled.

### Primary use of the kofta bake / cool window
Move directly into Phase 4.

### Checkpoint
The kofta should be browned at the edges, fully cooked, and cool enough that it will not steam the lavash.

### If needed
- *If the mixture feels loose or smear-y before shaping, chill 10 minutes longer; it is fixed when a narrow log holds without sagging immediately.*
- *If the baked kofta release a lot of liquid, leave them on the rack 5 to 10 minutes longer; it is fixed when the surfaces look dry enough to handle.*

---

## Phase 4 — Roll and pre-freeze the burritos
**Estimated time:** 20 to 30 min rolling + 5 min seam set + 20 to 30 min pre-freeze

### What you are doing
You are locking in the burritos while the kofta cool.

### Actions
1. Warm the 5 tortillas in the **Microwave / convection oven combo** for 10 to 15 seconds under a barely damp paper towel, just until flexible.
2. On each tortilla:
   - spread about 1 1/2 Tbsp refried beans in a narrow strip,
   - add about 1/2 cup cooled burrito filling in a short, flat log.
3. Fold the sides in and roll tightly.
4. Place each burrito seam-side down on the burrito tray.
5. Let them sit seam-side down 5 minutes.
6. Move the tray to the freezer for 20 to 30 minutes to set the seam and firm the outside.

### Primary use of the burrito pre-freeze window
Start Phase 5, the pupusa filling.

### Checkpoint
The burritos should be moderately thick, evenly packed, and not trying to spring open.

### If needed
- *If a tortilla cracks, warm the stack 5 seconds more; it is fixed when it folds without splitting.*
- *If a burrito feels thick and squat, remove 1 to 2 Tbsp filling and reroll; it is fixed when the center thickness is moderate and the seam stays closed.*

---

## Phase 5 — Make the pupusa filling
**Estimated time:** 15 to 20 min cook + 10 to 12 min cooling

### What you are doing
You are using the burrito pre-freeze block for the pupusa meat filling.

### Actions
1. In the **Cast iron skillet**, add the pupusa beef and **Brown** over **medium-high heat** for 5 to 7 minutes, breaking it into very small crumbles.
2. Add about 1/2 tsp avocado oil only if the pan looks dry.
3. Add:
   - diced pupusa potato
   - diced carrots
4. **Saute** 4 to 6 minutes until the edges start to soften.
5. Add:
   - 1 1/2 tsp tomato paste
   - 1 tsp cumin
   - 1 tsp sweet paprika
   - 1/2 tsp dried oregano
   - 1/4 tsp black pepper
   - 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
6. **Cook** 1 to 2 minutes, stirring, until the tomato paste darkens slightly.
7. Add 1 to 2 Tbsp water, lower to **medium heat**, and **Simmer** 2 to 4 minutes until the vegetables finish softening and the pan looks nearly dry.
8. Spread the finished filling onto a tray or shallow dish.
9. Cool 10 to 12 minutes, until warm at most.

### Primary use of the pupusa filling cooling window
Immediately begin Phase 6 and mix the masa.

### Checkpoint
The pupusa filling should hold together in a spoon and look tight, spoonable, and no longer weepy.

### If needed
- *If the pan still looks glossy with free liquid, cook 1 to 2 minutes more uncovered; it is fixed when the filling looks compact and dry enough to stuff.*
- *If the potatoes are still firm but the pan is dry, add 1 tsp water, cover 1 minute, uncover, and dry it back down; it is fixed when the potato yields when pressed.*

---

## Phase 6 — Mix and rest the pupusa masa
**Estimated time:** 5 min mix + 15 min rest

### What you are doing
You are using the pupusa filling cool window the way the recipe wants: masa hydrates while the filling rests.

### Actions
1. In a bowl, combine:
   - 3 cups masa harina
   - 1/2 tsp fine sea salt
2. Add 2 1/4 cups hot water and knead 1 to 2 minutes until smooth.
3. Let the masa rest at least 15 minutes.
4. After the rest, test one piece by flattening it in your palm.
5. If needed, adjust:
   - add warm water 1 Tbsp at a time if the edges crack badly,
   - add 1 to 2 tsp masa harina if it feels sticky and slumps.

### Primary use of the masa rest window
Move to Phase 7: bag the burritos and assemble the kofta wraps.

### Checkpoint
The masa should feel like soft Play-Doh: pliable, not crumbly, not aggressively sticky.

---

## Phase 7 — Bag the burritos and assemble the kofta wraps
**Estimated time:** 20 to 30 min combined

### A. Finish the burritos
1. Pull the burritos from the freezer once the outsides feel firm.
2. Wrap each in parchment.
3. Transfer to freezer bags.
4. Return the bagged burritos to the freezer **flat** until fully frozen.
5. Stack or store upright only after they are fully firm.

### B. Kofta sauce — default later, optional now
1. **Default path:** skip the sauce during the main freezer-prep session.
   - The wraps freeze well, but the yogurt sauce does not belong inside the freezer packet.
   - This is the better default unless you expect to eat some wraps within the next 48 hours.
2. **Optional near-term sauce path:** if you want sauce now, **Whisk** together:
   - 3/4 cup Greek yogurt
   - 1 tsp lemon juice
   - remaining scant 1/8 tsp kosher salt
3. Taste and add a few more drops of lemon only if needed.
4. Transfer the sauce to a separate fridge container.
5. Do **not** put it inside the wraps.
6. Do **not** freeze it inside the wrap packet.
7. Use it within about 2 days.

### C. Wrap the kofta
1. Warm each lavash portion briefly in the **Cast iron skillet** over **medium heat** only if it feels stiff or crack-prone.
2. Place 2 kofta logs on each wrap portion.
3. Add only a very small pinch of parsley inside if you want it.
4. Do **not** add yogurt sauce inside the wrap.
5. Fold tightly and wrap each piece in parchment or foil.
6. Transfer the wrapped kofta to freezer bags.

### Checkpoint
By the end of this phase, the burritos and kofta wraps should both be fully wrapped and bagged. If you made the optional yogurt sauce, it should be in the fridge separately.

### If needed
- *If the lavash dries instead of softening, lower the heat and shorten contact time; it is fixed when the bread bends without cracking.*

---

## Phase 8 — Shape the pupusas
**Estimated time:** 15 to 20 min

### What you are doing
You are turning the cooled pupusa filling and rested masa into 10 shaped pupusas before any griddling begins.

### Actions
1. Divide the masa into 10 equal balls.
2. Divide the filling into 10 portions.
3. Wet your hands lightly in the water-oil bowl.
4. Flatten one masa ball into a thick disk and press a shallow cup into the center.
5. Add one filling portion.
6. Pull the masa up around the filling, pinch closed, and gently flatten into a 4 1/2 to 5-in round about 1/2-in thick.
7. Place on the **Sheet / roasting trays**.
8. Repeat until all 10 are shaped.

### Checkpoint
Each pupusa should feel sealed, evenly thick, and slightly damp on the outside rather than dusty or dry.

### If needed
- *If a seam opens, patch it with a pea-sized piece of fresh masa and flatten again; it is fixed when no filling is visible.*
- *If the pupusa bulges in one spot, rotate and press from the center outward with lighter pressure; it is fixed when thickness looks even.*

---

## Phase 9 — Griddle, cool, and pack the pupusas
**Estimated time:** 24 to 32 min griddling + 15 min cooling + 5 to 10 min packing

### What you are doing
You are cooking the pupusas in batches and packing them once they are fully set and cooled.

### Actions
1. Reheat the **Cast iron skillet** over **medium heat** for 2 to 3 minutes.
2. Add a thin film of avocado oil.
3. **Cook** the pupusas in batches of 2 to 3, 3 to 4 minutes per side, until set and lightly browned in spots.
4. Expect about 4 batches total.
5. Transfer the cooked pupusas to the tray and cool at least 15 minutes before packing.
6. Pack 2 pupusas per packet, using parchment between layers if stacking.

### Checkpoint
The pupusas should be fully set, lightly browned, and not gummy inside when you tear open the test one.

### If needed
- *If the center is gummy, lower the heat slightly and add 30 to 60 seconds per side on the next batch; it is fixed when the center feels set when torn open.*
- *If browning is racing before the center sets, the pan is too hot; it is fixed when the surface browns lightly over the full 3 to 4 minute side.*

# Packing labels
- **Kofta Wraps** — 1 wrap each — microwave from frozen; add yogurt after reheating only if you made or mix the sauce later
- **Picadillo Burritos** — 1 burrito each — microwave lower and slower; rest before eating
- **Pupusas** — 2 per packet — microwave covered or in damp towel

---

# Reheat instructions

## Kofta wraps
- Microwave from frozen under a lightly damp paper towel for about **2 1/2 to 4 minutes at 70 to 80 percent power**, flipping halfway, until the center is steaming hot.
- Rest 1 minute.
- Add yogurt sauce after reheating.

## Burritos
- From frozen, wrap in a slightly damp paper towel and microwave at **50 percent power for 2 minutes**, flip, then **50 percent power for 2 minutes**, then **70 percent power for 30 to 90 seconds if needed**.
- Rest 1 to 2 minutes before eating.

## Pupusas
- From frozen, microwave at **70 to 80 percent power for about 4 to 5 minutes**, covered or wrapped in a damp paper towel, until the center is steaming hot.
- Rest covered briefly so the corn dough softens fully instead of feeling leathery.
- If the center is hot but the edges feel tough, let them sit covered 15 to 20 seconds before adding more microwave time.

# Storage windows

## Kofta wraps
- **Best freezer path:** freeze the assembled wraps the day you build them.
- **Freezer hold:** about **2 months** for best quality.
- **Fridge note:** the cooked kofta themselves can hold about **3 days refrigerated** before wrapping if you want to delay assembly.
- **Sauce note:** optional yogurt sauce is a short fridge item; use within about **2 days**.

## Burritos
- **Rolled burritos in the fridge:** about **3 to 4 days**.
- **Freezer hold:** about **2 months** for best quality.
- **Best texture:** thaw overnight in the fridge when practical, but direct-from-frozen microwave reheat is built into the recipe.

## Pupusas
- **Cooked pupusas in the fridge:** about **3 to 4 days**.
- **Freezer hold:** about **2 to 3 months** for best quality.
- **Best freeze point:** after griddling and full cooling, packed 2 per packet.

# Failure points to watch during the session
Do **not** cut these corners:
- Do **not** roll burritos while the filling is still steaming.
- Do **not** wrap kofta while the kofta is still hot.
- Do **not** shape pupusas with wet filling.
- Do **not** crowd the skillet during pupusa griddling.
- Do **not** put yogurt sauce inside the freezer wraps.

---

# Best pause points
If you need to stop without wrecking the plan:
1. **After the burrito filling is cooled** — refrigerate it and roll later. **Hold:** about **2 days** in the fridge before rolling.
2. **After the kofta are baked and cooled** — refrigerate them and wrap later. **Hold:** about **3 days** in the fridge before wrapping.
3. **After the pupusa filling is cooked and cooled** — refrigerate it and shape / cook later. **Hold:** about **2 days** in the fridge before shaping.

These are the safest stop points because they preserve the recipes’ cooling and assembly logic instead of interrupting them mid-shape or mid-wrap.
