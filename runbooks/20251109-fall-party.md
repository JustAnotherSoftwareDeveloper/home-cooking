# 3-Day Fall Party Runbook (Single Oven + Instant Pot)

> **Brunch (ready by 9:00 AM):** Overnight Spinach & Cheese Strata · Mixed Greens w/ Pear, Cranberry & Toasted Nuts · Apple-Cider “Doughnut” Snack Cake  
> **Lunch (serve at 1:30 PM):** Cheerwine-Glazed Instant Pot Meatballs · Roasted-Garlic & Fontina Stuffed Cheesy Bread

---

## Day -2 (Shopping + Long-Lead Prep | ~90 min hands-on)

- [ ] Shop from the consolidated list; bin items by dish (fridge & pantry).
- [ ] **Cheesy Bread Dough (cold-ferment start, 24–72 h):** Mix/knead; refrigerate. (Plan to pull 60–90 min before baking on Day 0.)
- [ ] **Roast Garlic + Make Garlic-Herb Butter:** Roast 45–60 min @ 375°F until pale-gold; mash into butter; refrigerate (or freeze).
- [ ] **Salad Do-Ahead:**
  - [ ] Toast nuts 7–8 min @ 350°F; cool and container.
  - [ ] Whisk dressing (1/4 cup white balsamic, 1 tsp Dijon, 1–2 tsp honey, ~1/2 cup oil, salt/pepper); refrigerate.
- [ ] **Optional (if bread is very fresh):** Cube strata bread and dry 10–15 min @ 300°F; cool.

---

## Day -1 (Do-Ahead Builds | ~2.5–3 h hands-on)

- [ ] **Bake Snack Cake:** 28–35 min @ 350°F. Leave **unsugared**; wrap airtight at room temp. (Tomorrow: warm 8–10 min @ 300°F, then butter + cinnamon-sugar.)
- [ ] **Assemble Strata:** Sauté onions; wring spinach dry; layer with cheeses/bread. Whisk custard; pour, cover, and **refrigerate 8–24 h**.
- [ ] **Meatballs Do-Ahead:**
  - [ ] Reduce Cheerwine; simmer with ketchup, ACV, spices to **glossy glaze**; chill.
  - [ ] Mix & shape meatballs (1–1.25"): cover and **refrigerate ≤24 h**.
  - [ ] Prep IP: oil trivet; measure thin liquid (**1 cup for 6-qt / 1.5 cups for 8-qt**).
- [ ] **Cheesy Bread Staging:** If not done Day -2, roast garlic & finish butter; refrigerate.

---

## Day 0 — Brunch Block (Everything ready by **9:00 AM**)

**7:00 AM**
- [ ] Pull **strata** from fridge to take the chill off.
- [ ] Unwrap **snack cake** (for reheating/finishing).
- [ ] Place salad greens in bowl with paper towel; set dressing/nuts nearby.

**7:15 AM — Preheat**
- [ ] Oven to **350°F** (center rack).

**7:30–8:25 AM — Bake Strata (50–60 min)**
- [ ] Finish topping.
- [ ] **Foil first 30 min**, then **uncover** to finish.
- [ ] Doneness: center puffed, edges browned, **≥165°F** in middle.

**8:25–8:40 AM — Rest Strata (10–15 min)**
- [ ] Move to rack; rest for clean slices.

**8:40–8:55 AM — Warm Cake + Toss Salad**
- [ ] Lower oven to **300°F**; **warm cake 8–10 min**.
- [ ] Brush cake with melted butter; coat with cinnamon-sugar; set on rack 2–3 min.
- [ ] Slice pears; toss greens with **~1/2 cup dressing**; add cranberries + toasted nuts.

**9:00 AM — Brunch Out**
- [ ] Serve strata + salad; plate cake.

> **Holding tips:** Strata slices hold **20–30 min @ 200°F** under loose foil. Dress salad close to service for best texture.

---

## Day 0 — Lunch Block (Serve at **1:30 PM**)

**12:00 PM — Dough & Glaze to Room Temp**
- [ ] Pull **cheesy-bread dough** from fridge for **60–90 min** bench rest.
- [ ] Bring **meatball glaze** to room temp.
- [ ] Add thin liquid to IP (**1 cup 6-qt / 1.5 cups 8-qt**).

**12:45 PM — Preheat for Bread**
- [ ] Oven to **450°F**.
- [ ] If using a baking **steel/stone**, preheat it **30–45 min**.

**1:00 PM — Start Meatballs (Instant Pot)**
- [ ] Load meatballs; **High Pressure 9 min** + **Natural Release 5 min**; check **160°F**.
- [ ] Toss with hot glaze (**Sauté 1–2 min** to lacquer).
- [ ] Hold on **Keep Warm (≥140°F)**.

**1:05–1:15 PM — Shape Cheesy Bread**
- [ ] Roll to **~10×14"**, spread roasted-garlic cheese filling, fold/seal, cut **vent slits**; transfer to sheet.

**~1:14–1:18 PM — Bake Cheesy Bread**
- [ ] **12–16 min @ 450°F** to deep golden.
- [ ] Finish with **garlic butter + Parmesan** (optional extra 1–2 min).
- [ ] Rest **5 min**, then slice.

**1:30–1:35 PM — Lunch Out**
- [ ] Set out **Cheerwine meatballs** (glossy, hot) and **cheesy garlic bread**.
- [ ] *If bread finished early:* hold **30–45 min @ 200°F** on a rack-lined sheet; re-brush butter before serving.

---

## Between Brunch & Lunch (9:15–11:45 AM) — Optional Tidy-Ups

- [ ] Reset work surfaces; stage serving platters, tongs, ladles/spoons.
- [ ] Pre-chop parsley/scallions for garnishes.
- [ ] Confirm oven rack positions for 450°F bake.

---

## Gear & Safety Notes

- **Garlic-Herb Butter:** Store refrigerated (or frozen), not at room temp.
- **Instant Pot:** Always use **1 cup (6-qt) / 1.5 cups (8-qt)** thin liquid to avoid “Burn.”
- **Cheesy Bread Browning:** A **metal pan** and/or baking on a preheated **steel/stone** improves bottom color.
