# Top Round, Brown Rice & Black Bean Freezer Burritos (One-Bowl, No Peppers/Onions)

## Title & Overview
Lean **top round roast** (sliced, bite-size, hot-seared) for protein, **brown rice** for steady energy, and **thickened black beans** for fiber and structure. Flavor comes from a **chipotles-in-adobo mayo** folded into the mix (half) and saved (half) for serving. All fillings **cool before mixing** to prevent soggy wraps after freezing; burritos are flash-frozen for clean reheating [1].

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: 12 burritos (10 in tortillas); leftovers by default
- Active Prep: ~60 minutes (excluding cooling)
- Inactive Prep: cool fillings 20–30 minutes; flash-freeze ~60 minutes
- Cook: ~30 minutes total (rice hands-off; seared beef + beans)
- Make-ahead: Cooked beef/rice/beans keep 3–4 days in the fridge; assembled burritos keep up to ~3 months for best quality. Always reheat burritos until **steaming-hot in the center** before serving [1].

## Ingredients
(Listed in use order. Mark advance-prep items with [pre-prep].)

**Chipotle Mayo (with chipotles in adobo) [pre-prep]**
- Mayonnaise, 1 cup (240 ml)
- Chipotle peppers from the can, 2–3, very finely minced, plus 1–2 Tbsp adobo sauce
- **Lime juice, 2–3 tsp**
- Optional: small pinch cayenne

**Brown Rice (rice cooker) [pre-prep]**
- Brown rice, 2 cups raw (cook per your rice cooker)
- Garlic powder, 1/2 tsp
- Onion powder, 1/4 tsp
- Kosher salt, 1/2–1 tsp, to taste
- **Lime juice, 2–3 tsp**

**Seared Top Round (bite-size) [pre-prep]**
- Top round roast, 2.5 lb (1130 g), trimmed and sliced bite-size
- Olive oil, 1 Tbsp (plus more as needed)
- Garlic, 2 cloves, minced
- Ground cumin, 1 1/2 tsp
- Smoked paprika, 1 tsp
- Kosher salt, 3/4–1 tsp
- Black pepper, 1/2 tsp
- **Lime juice, 1–2 tsp, to finish**

**Thickened Black Beans [pre-prep]**
- Olive oil, 1 Tbsp
- Garlic, 1 clove, minced
- Black beans, 2–3 x 15 oz cans, drained, rinsed, patted very dry [4]
- No-salt tomato paste, 2 Tbsp
- Ground cumin, 1/2 tsp
- Smoked paprika, 1/4 tsp
- Kosher salt, 1/4–1/2 tsp, to taste
- Water or reserved bean liquid, 2–4 Tbsp, as needed

**To Assemble [pre-prep]**
- Flour tortillas, 12, 10 in
- Thick salsa, 1 to 1 1/2 cups (low-watery)
- Reserved chipotle mayo (about 1/2 cup) for serving

## Detailed Instructions
1. **Cook and season rice (hands-off).** Rinse and cook **2 cups raw brown rice** in your rice cooker. Fluff; let steam off 1–2 minutes. Toss with garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and **2–3 tsp lime juice**. Spread on a sheet pan to **cool 10–15 minutes** so steam does not soften tortillas later [1].

2. **Make chipotle mayo.** Stir minced chipotles + adobo into **1 cup mayo** with **2–3 tsp lime juice** (optional pinch cayenne). Cover and chill **>=30 minutes** to bloom.

3. **Sear the top round.** Heat a large skillet over **medium-high**. Add oil; when shimmering, add sliced beef in batches so it **sizzles, not steams**. Sear to browned edges and just-cooked centers (quick, hot tosses). Add garlic; cook 30 sec. Season with cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper; cook 1 min. Off heat, finish with **1–2 tsp lime juice**. Spread on a tray to **cool 10–15 min**.

4. **Thicken the beans.** Over **medium** heat, warm oil; saute garlic 30 sec. Stir in tomato paste; cook to brick-red 1–2 min. Add **well-dried** beans, cumin, smoked paprika, and salt; mash **about half** the beans as they heat. Add only enough liquid to reach a **thick, spreadable** consistency. Transfer to a shallow bowl; **cool 10–15 min**. Rinsing canned beans helps reduce sodium [4].

5. **One-bowl mix (your method).** In a large bowl combine:
   - All cooled rice
   - All cooled seared beef
   - 2–3 cans thickened black beans (to your preference)
   - 1 cup thick salsa
   - 1/2 cup chipotle mayo (reserve the other 1/2 cup for serving)
   - **2–3 tsp lime juice**; salt/pepper to taste  
   Fold just to combine. Aim for **thick and scoopable**, not wet. If loose, add rice; if dry, add 1–2 Tbsp mayo or a spoon of bean liquid. Chill **10–15 min** to lightly firm before rolling.

6. **Warm tortillas.** Stack and wrap in a **damp towel**; microwave 20–30 sec until pliable. Keep covered as you work.

7. **Assemble and roll.** Divide bowl mix into **12 equal mounds** (~1 cup each). Place a mound on each tortilla, shape into a compact log, fold sides in, and **roll tightly**; press seam down. (Optional: smear 1 tsp reserved mayo inside before rolling.)

8. **Flash-freeze and wrap.** Place seam-side-down on a parchment-lined sheet; freeze ~60 min until firm. Wrap tightly (plastic, then foil); label/date. Best quality within ~3 months [1].

9. **Reheat from frozen (choose one).**
   - Microwave, softest: Unwrap. Wrap in a **damp paper towel**; microwave 2 1/2–3 1/2 min, flipping halfway; rest 1 min [2].
   - Microwave + skillet: Microwave 2–3 min (slightly under). Sear in a lightly oiled skillet 1 min/side.
   - Air fryer: 350 deg F 10–14 min, turn once. If center lags, microwave 60–90 sec first, then air-fry 3–4 min.
   - Oven, batch: 375 deg F 25–30 min on parchment; tent with foil for 15 min, then uncover to crisp. Reheat to **steaming-hot in the center** [1].


## Special Notes & Variations
- Storage & Reheat: Components hold 3–4 days refrigerated; assembled burritos best within ~3 months frozen. Always reheat to **steaming-hot in the center** [1]. The **damp paper towel** microwave trick helps even heating and prevents dried edges [2].
- Serving ideas: After reheating, add a small splash of **lime juice**; serve with a little reserved chipotle mayo. Optional sour cream or avocado on the side (do not freeze avocado inside).
- Dietary toggles:
  - Lower sodium: low-sodium tortillas/salsa; rinse beans very well [4]; keep mayo light.
  - Higher protein: increase beef portion per burrito; reduce rice slightly.
  - Dairy-free: use an egg-free, dairy-free mayo alternative.
