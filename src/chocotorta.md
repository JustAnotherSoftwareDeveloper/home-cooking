# Chocotorta

## Title & Overview
Argentina’s beloved no-bake icebox cake: layers of thin chocolate biscuits briefly dipped in cold, unsweetened espresso, spread with a glossy 1:1 blend of dulce de leche repostero and cream cheese, then capped with a thin dark‑chocolate ganache. Built to chill overnight for tidy, cafe‑worthy slices and a clean, not‑too‑sweet finish.

## Ingredients
- Instant espresso powder — **1 to 1¼ tsp**
- Cold water — **¾ to 1 cup (180–240 ml)** (for dipping biscuits)
- Cream cheese, softened — **16 oz (450 g)**
- Dulce de leche repostero — **16 oz (≈450 g)**
- Vanilla extract — **1 tsp**
- Kosher salt — **¼ tsp** (use **⅛ tsp** if Morton)
- Chocolinas biscuits — **~16–22 per layer, 3–4 layers total** (~14–18 oz / 400–500 g overall)
- Dark chocolate (70–75%) — **4 oz (115 g)** (ganache)
- Heavy cream — **3 oz (90 ml)** (ganache)
- Cocoa powder or shaved dark chocolate (optional, to finish)

## Prep Gotchas & QA
- Dip each biscuit ~1 second per side; keep the dip **cold** and **unsweetened** to avoid soggy, cloying layers.
- Use pastry‑grade dulce de leche (**repostero**) for tidy layers that resist weeping; regular dulce works with longer chill.
- Filling texture should be thick, spreadable, and glossy; loosen with 1–2 Tbsp milk/cream if too stiff.
- Let ganache cool to ~95°F/35°C before pouring so it doesn’t melt the top layer.
- Chill fully **12–24 hours** for clean slices; wipe the knife between cuts.

## Detailed Instructions
1) Brew the cold espresso dip (2 min). In a measuring cup, whisk **1–1¼ tsp** instant espresso into **¾ cup (180 ml)** cold water until dissolved (20–30 sec). Taste: bold/roasty, not harsh. If too strong, add **2–4 Tbsp** cold water (up to **1 cup / 240 ml** total). Keep cold.
2) Make the filling (3–5 min). Beat **cream cheese (16 oz / 450 g)** on medium until completely smooth, **45–60 sec**; scrape the bowl. Add **dulce de leche repostero (16 oz / 450 g)**, **1 tsp** vanilla, and **¼ tsp** kosher salt; beat **60–90 sec** until glossy and homogeneous.
   - Texture check: thick, spreadable, soft ridges. Too stiff? Mix in **1–2 Tbsp** milk/cream. Too loose? Beat in **1–2 Tbsp** more repostero or chill the bowl **10 min**.
3) Stage the pan (2 min). Line an **8×8** pan with parchment, leaving long overhangs for lifting. Set up an assembly line: pan → espresso dip → biscuits → offset spatula → filling.
4) Lay the base (3–4 min). Working one at a time, dip each biscuit ~1 second per side, lift, shake off excess, and tile snugly to cover the bottom (trim to fit corners). Expect **~16–22 biscuits** per layer.
5) First filling layer (1–2 min). Dollop and spread **⅓** of the filling evenly to the edges. Don’t compress—keep it airy for a tender bite.
6) Repeat layers (6–8 min). Build **Biscuits → Filling → Biscuits → Filling → Biscuits → Filling**, finishing with **filling on top** for a smooth surface. Most 8×8 builds are **3 biscuit layers + 3 filling layers**. If biscuits are thicker, **3 biscuit layers + 2 filling layers** also works—still finish with filling. Run a clean spatula around edges for straight sides.
7) Make the ganache cap (5–7 min, mostly hands‑off). Put **4 oz (115 g)** finely chopped dark chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Heat **3 oz (90 ml)** heavy cream just to steaming; pour over chocolate. Cover **2 min**, then whisk from the center until silky. Let cool to **lukewarm (~95°F / 35°C)**.
8) Finish the top (1–2 min). Pour ganache over the cake and tilt the pan to level. Tap gently to release bubbles. Optional: after the ganache sets, dust lightly with **cocoa** or add **shaved dark chocolate**.
9) Cover & chill (overnight). Press plastic wrap gently onto the surface; wrap the pan. Refrigerate **12–24 hours** so the biscuits hydrate and the filling/ganache set.
10) Unmold & slice (5 min). Lift the slab via the parchment overhangs. For sharp edges, dip a knife in **hot water**, wipe dry, and cut straight down, wiping between cuts. Portion: 3×3 = **9** generous squares; 3×4 = **12** party squares.

## Allergy & Dietary Notes
- **Contains:** milk, wheat (cookies). May contain soy (lecithin) depending on chocolate brand.
- **Swaps:** gluten-free chocolate wafers; lactose‑free cream cheese; choose chocolate without soy lecithin if needed.
- **Cross-contact:** If serving to guests with allergies, verify cookie and chocolate labels.

## Scaling & Batch Size
- 9×13-inch pan: **1.5×** the recipe for modest height; **2×** for taller layers (watch chill time).
- 9×9-inch pan: **~1.2×** the recipe.
- Mini desserts: build in individual ramekins or glasses; shorten chill slightly.

## Make-Ahead, Storage & Reheat (Optional)
- **Fridge:** Keeps **3–4 days** covered and cold; slices cleaner on day 2.
- **Freezer:** Freeze well‑wrapped up to **1 month**; thaw overnight in fridge.
- **Reheat:** Not applicable.

## Serving & Variations
- Serve with: fresh berries, lightly sweetened whipped cream, or a sprinkle of cocoa.
- Variations:
  - A little more tang: swap **2–3 Tbsp** of the cream cheese for **sour cream** (keep total “cheese” weight at 16 oz / 450 g).
  - No Chocolinas: use other thin **chocolate tea biscuits** or plain **chocolate wafers** of similar thickness (avoid cream‑filled cookies).
  - Coffee‑free: dip in cold milk with a touch of cocoa (sweeter; reduce dulce slightly if desired).

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Likely Cause | Quick Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Slices too soft | Biscuits over‑soaked; regular dulce too loose | Chill longer; freeze 10–12 min before slicing; next time dip faster and/or use all repostero |
| Bitter coffee taste | Espresso dip mixed too strong | Dilute with cold water; target bold but not harsh |
| Ganache melts top | Ganache poured too warm | Let ganache cool to ~95°F/35°C before pouring |

## Safety & Correctness Notes (if any)
Keep dip **cold** and dips brief to avoid microbial growth and sogginess; use fresh dairy and handle under clean conditions.
