# No-Bake Potassium Oat, Date, and Almond Bites (Blackstrap Molasses)

## Title & Overview
These chewy, freezer-friendly bites taste like caramel-leaning oat-and-date dough with pops of apricot and pepita crunch. A small amount of blackstrap molasses adds depth and a little extra mineral content.[1] This is low-sodium by construction (no added salt) and leans on fruits, whole grains, and nuts/seeds in line with broad USDA healthy eating guidance.[2] Rolling and freezing makes portioning simple and lets you thaw only what you need.[3]

## Yield & Timing
- Servings: about 20 bites (heaping tablespoon portions; about 1.2 oz / 34 g each)
- Active prep: about 20 minutes
- Inactive prep: 30 to 60 minutes to firm in freezer
- Cook: 0 (no-bake)
- Total: about 50 to 80 minutes

Make-ahead:
- Fridge: up to 1 week airtight
- Freezer: up to 1 month airtight; thaw 5 to 10 minutes at room temp before eating.[3]

## Ingredients
1. Medjool dates, pitted: 12 large (about 10 oz / 285 g) [pre-prep]
2. Banana: 1 medium (about 4 oz / 118 g)
3. Almond butter, unsalted: 1/2 cup (8 Tbsp / 128 g)
4. Vanilla extract: 1 tsp (about 4 g)
5. Blackstrap molasses: 1 tsp (about 7 g)
6. Rolled oats (old-fashioned): 2 cups (about 160 g)
7. Chia seeds: 2 Tbsp (about 1 oz / 28 g)
8. Ground flaxseed: 1 Tbsp (about 1/4 oz / 7 g)
9. Pepitas (pumpkin seeds), unsalted: 1/4 cup (about 1 oz / 28 g) [pre-prep]
10. Dried apricots: 1/2 cup, chopped (about 3 oz / 85 g) [pre-prep]
11. Ground cinnamon: 1/2 tsp (optional)
12. Avocado oil spray: optional, for lightly greasing hands

## Detailed Instructions
1. Line a sheet pan with parchment and clear space in your freezer.
   - If your kitchen is warm, chill the sheet pan 10 minutes to help the bites set faster.

2. (Optional) If dates are firm: cover with hot water for 5 minutes, then drain very well and squeeze out excess water.

3. In a food processor, pulse dates into a sticky paste (30 to 60 seconds). Scrape down as needed.

4. Add banana, almond butter, vanilla, and molasses. Process until thick and cohesive.

5. Add oats, chia, flax, and cinnamon (if using). Pulse 10 to 15 times, just until combined (do not grind into flour).

6. Transfer mixture to a bowl. Fold in pepitas and chopped apricots. Rest 5 minutes so oats/chia hydrate.

7. Portion and roll:
   - Lightly oil your hands (avocado oil spray) or dampen them with water.
   - Scoop heaping tablespoon portions and roll into about 20 balls. Place on the sheet pan with a small gap between bites.

8. Freeze 30 to 60 minutes, until the outside feels firm and bites lift cleanly. Transfer to an airtight container or freezer bag.

Make-Ahead Map:
- Steps 2-4 up to 3 days ahead: store date paste airtight in the fridge, then continue later.
- Step 8: freeze up to 1 month; thaw 5 to 10 minutes before eating.[3]

## Special Notes & Variations
- Texture fixes:
  - Too sticky to roll: mix in 1 to 2 Tbsp additional oats, then chill the bowl 10 minutes.
  - Too dry/crumbly: mix in warm water 1 tsp at a time (usually 1 to 3 tsp total) until it holds together when squeezed.
- Low-sodium guardrail: confirm almond butter and pepitas are explicitly "unsalted"; salted versions will materially change sodium.
- Flavor options that do not add sodium:
  - Increase cinnamon to 1 tsp for a more "spiced cookie" profile.
  - Add 1/8 tsp ground ginger or 1/2 tsp orange zest.
