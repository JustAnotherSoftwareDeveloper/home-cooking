# Egg, Swiss, Beef and Mushroom Slab Sandwich Squares (mix-and-pour v2.1)

## Title & Overview
All-in-one breakfast-lunch squares designed to eat as-is or tuck into sandwiches. Flavor leans savory and beefy with mushroom umami, boosted by a Swiss-Gruyere blend plus a small hit of tomato paste and miso. Assembly is one-bowl: mix, pour, bake. We target 160 deg F in the center for safe doneness. [1]

## Tags

## Yield & Timing
- Yield: 12 squares (designed to leave leftovers)
- Active Prep: 25 to 30 minutes
- Inactive Prep: 20 to 30 minutes (cooling and brief chill)
- Cook: 22 to 30 min in a light metal 9x13 at 325 deg F, or 30 to 42 min in glass or ceramic 9x13 at 300 deg F
- Total: about 1 hour 15 minutes
- Make-ahead: Refrigerate cooked squares 3 to 4 days; freeze for best quality up to about 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge or reheat from frozen; reheat leftovers until the center reaches 165 deg F. [2]

## Ingredients
- Avocado oil or spray, for pan
- Ground beef, 1.25 lb (90 to 93 percent lean), browned and well-drained, cooled [pre-prep]
- Cremini mushrooms, 16 oz, finely chopped and sauteed until most moisture evaporates, cooled [pre-prep]
- Scallion greens or chives, 1 cup loosely packed, thinly sliced [pre-prep]
- Large eggs, 14
- Milk, 1 cup
- Cornstarch, 1 Tbsp
- Dijon mustard, 1 Tbsp
- Worcestershire sauce or coconut aminos, 1 to 2 tsp
- Tomato paste, 1 tsp
- Swiss cheese, 8 oz, shredded [pre-prep]
- Gruyere, 4 oz, shredded [pre-prep]
- Mushroom powder, 1 to 2 tsp (optional but recommended)
- White miso, 1 tsp
- Dried sage, 2 tsp
- Dried thyme, 1 tsp
- Fennel seed, 1 tsp, lightly crushed
- Smoked paprika, 1 tsp
- Black pepper, 1 tsp
- Ground nutmeg, 1/4 tsp
- Lemon zest, to taste (optional finish)

## Detailed Instructions
1) Heat oven and prep pan
Place a rack in the center. For a light-colored metal 9x13 pan, heat to 325 deg F. For glass or ceramic 9x13, heat to 300 deg F. Glass and ceramic heat more slowly and can run hotter at the edges, so lowering about 25 deg F and baking a bit longer helps the center set without over-browning the perimeter. [5][6] Lightly oil the pan and line with a parchment sling if you want easy lifting.

2) Cook beef and mushrooms
Warm a large skillet over medium heat. Cook the beef, breaking into small bits, until no longer pink and lightly browned, 7 to 9 minutes. Drain very well. Stir in the tomato paste and cook 60 to 90 seconds until brick-red and aromatic. Add the chopped mushrooms and cook, stirring, until they release moisture and the pan looks steamy-dry with gentle sizzle, 5 to 7 minutes. Spread on a plate to cool 5 minutes.

3) Mix-everything bowl
In a cup, whisk the milk with cornstarch until smooth, then whisk in the white miso to fully dissolve. In a very large bowl, whisk the eggs until uniform. Whisk in the miso-milk, then whisk in Dijon, Worcestershire or aminos, mushroom powder, and all spices (sage, thyme, fennel, smoked paprika, pepper, nutmeg). Fold in the cooled beef-mushroom mixture and the scallions or chives. Fold in about two-thirds of the cheese mix (about 8 oz total, for example 6 oz Swiss plus 2 oz Gruyere). Cornstarch helps the custard stay tender through chill and reheat. [3][4]

4) Pan, top, and bake
Lightly oil the pan if not already oiled. Pour the mixture into the pan and scatter the remaining cheese evenly over the top.
- Light metal 9x13 at 325 deg F: start checking at 22 minutes; typical finish 22 to 30 minutes.
- Glass or ceramic 9x13 at 300 deg F: start checking at 30 minutes; typical finish 30 to 42 minutes.
The center should be just set with a slight wobble; a thin knife near the center comes out mostly clean, and an instant-read thermometer inserted in the center reads 160 deg F. [1] Rotate the pan if one side browns faster.

5) Cool, cut, chill, and store
Cool in the pan on a rack 10 to 15 minutes. Loosen edges, lift with parchment, and transfer to a board. Optional: microplane a little lemon zest over the surface. Slice into 12 squares (3 by 4 grid). Rapid-chill on a sheet pan in the fridge 10 to 15 minutes. Wrap individually and refrigerate 3 to 4 days or freeze for best quality up to about 3 months. Reheat leftovers, covered, until the center reaches 165 deg F. [2]

- Make-Ahead Map: Steps 1 to 2 and any [pre-prep] items up to 3 days ahead (keep chilled). Step 4 bakes day-of. Cooked squares hold 3 to 4 days refrigerated or freeze for best quality up to about 3 months; reheat leftovers to 165 deg F. [2][1]

## Nutrition Snapshot (per serving)
Qualitative: high-quality protein from eggs, lean beef, and cheese; decent calcium from dairy; minimal added sugar. Sodium rises with cheese and Worcestershire or aminos, but you get strong flavor from mushroom powder, miso, tomato paste, herbs, and spices without increasing salt.

## Special Notes & Variations
- Storage and Reheat: Keep cooked squares refrigerated 3 to 4 days; freeze up to about 3 months for best quality. Reheat leftovers to 165 deg F. [2]
- Flavor dials that pair well with this base:
  - Fresh herb lift: add 1 Tbsp minced fresh thyme and 2 Tbsp extra chives at mixing.
  - Mild heat: add 1/2 tsp Aleppo pepper or 1/4 to 1/2 tsp chipotle powder.
  - Top caramelization: broil 30 to 60 seconds at the end; watch closely.
